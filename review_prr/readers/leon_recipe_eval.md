**In brief.**
- **Headline** (RECIPE.md, 2026-10-16): recipe `grpo_think_vs_mb_kkl0`, 3 seeds, plain reads, pass@1 / pass@5 in % of the
  set:

  | set | pass@1 / pass@5 |

- **Model and reads.** The model is a ≈9.6M-parameter per-step proof-state model (S1g3) with think blocks.
  - It is read with n = 16 / 16 / 8 / 64 / 64 attempts per problem, at T 0.8 and think budget B64, with eval sampling
    seed 0.

- **Judge.** Lean alone: core Lean 4.34.0 on the literal text of finished attempts. `nd_verify` is not called.
- **Plain and guided reads.** Plain reads have no retries; a step the environment rejects ends the attempt as a
  failure. Guided reads allow up to 10 redraws per attempt.

  - (a) textbook72 is a design set for the generator that made the RL pools. The dev58 gap analysis shaped that
    generator, and RECIPE.md does not mention this.
  - (b) The generated sets and Charles149 were used to select the final knob.
  - (c) Most tuning gains are about the size of the measured rerun noise.
  - (d) GRPO reached parity with (nearly untuned) EI on the generated sets only after about 15 GRPO pilots.

## 3. Headline numbers, one row per number as Leon reports it

### Legend (expanded once, referred to by code in the table)

- **Pv2, plain read (v2).**
  - `line.sh read1` runs `python -m code.thinking.eval --ckpt <L3 last ckpt> --sets … --k n --budgets 64 --arms plain
    --batch 1024 --seed 0 --temperature 0.8 --max_action 512 --max_steps 96 --max_rej 10`
    (`LM:TT/grpo_v2/launch/line.sh:L300-304`). `RB=64` for thinking arms (L195). `KS=(16 64 64 64 8)` (L196).
  - A step the environment rejects ends the attempt (`LM:code/thinking/sample.py:L33`). §4 has the details.
- **Gv2, guided read (v2).** Pv2 with `--arms logical`: a step the environment or `step_check` rejects is redrawn, up to
  10 redraws per attempt (`LM:code/thinking/sample.py:L33-35, L916, L944`).
- **J, the judge.** Lean alone: core Lean 4.34.0, no Mathlib, on the literal text of each finished attempt, through the
  vendored `lean_gate.gate` with a reject-only prefilter. `nd_verify` is not called (§4.4).
- **E, the estimator.** Unbiased pass@k = 1 − C(n−c, k)/C(n, k) per problem, from n attempts. The value is the mean over
  problems and seeds. Intervals are 95% hierarchical bootstrap over seeds and problems, 4,000 draws (§4.6).
- **M-S1g3, the Stage-1 init.**
  - Checkpoints `runs/stage1/S1g3_s{0,1,2}/model.pt`, retrained on ynez 2026-10-13 (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L70-72`).
  - Architecture: ALiBiGPT, "6 layers, d 384, 8 heads, d_ff 1280" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L61`), about 9.6M parameters
    (`LM:TT/OVERNIGHT_20261008.md:L217`: "| | 9.6M (2 seeds) | 35.6M (seed 0) |").
  - Interface: a per-step proof-state model with effort token. The input is `state <eB>`; the target is `<think> …
    </think> action <eos>` (`LM:code/thinking/train.py:L17-21`). The action is one Lean proof step, which the state
    environment applies.
  - Training: from scratch ("From scratch only", `train.py:L23`), 24,000 steps of Dan's best recipe.
  - Data: programmatic honest-search traces over the K12 theorems at levels 0 / 64 / 256, mixed 20 / 40 / 40, "with
    textbook72 excluded" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L56-57`). K12 proofs are 2–12 lines (`LM:TT/test_21_30/README.md:L20`:
    "K12 2–12 lines, median 7"). The search found "99.76% of K12 proofs" (`LM:TT/OVERNIGHT_20261008.md:L55`).
  - Context: max_len 1,024 (`LM:TT/search_traces/README.md:L433-434`).
- **M-KL0, the RL model.**
  - `grpo_think_vs_mb_kkl0_s{S}_L3`, last checkpoint `_it39`. L3 starts from `grpo_think_vs_s{S}_L2_it39.pt`
    (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L110-111`).
  - Rollbacks: "seed 0 once, on the canary at it 29, restored to it 25". Seed 1 rolled back twice, so its final weights
    are "it 30 plus one iteration at lr × 0.25" (L332-334).
- **R-KL0, the RL algorithm and compute.**
  - Algorithm: Dr. GRPO, "r minus the mean over the group's unmasked attempts (no division by the group std)"
    (`LM:code/thinking/grpo.py:L33-34`). Clip 0.2 / 0.28, dual clip 3, 4 updates per batch, training "sampling
    temperature 1.0" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L155-157`).
  - Reward: "1 if Lean accepts the whole proof, else 0" (`grpo.py:L17`).
  - Sampler: the stop arm, `--arm logical --max_rej 0 --rej_penalty 0.1` (`RECIPE.md:L104`). Budgets 0 / 64 / 256 with
    effort tokens; `--overlong fail`; `--max_steps 96` (L129-136).
  - **L2:** 39 iterations × 256 × 16 on the L2 pool (5,000 rows, 10–12 lines), KL 0.02 to S1g3 (L97-104, L128).
  - **L3:** 39 iterations × 256 × 16 on the L3 pool (5,000 rows, 13–15 lines), plus "a third of each iteration's groups"
    from 3-premise rewrites (1,843 rows). WSD 1−sqrt cooldown to 0.3×, unlikeliness β 0.25, KL 0 (L25-29, L110-120,
    L143).
  - Attempts: "A plain stage samples 39 × 4,096 = 159,744 attempts" (`LM:TT/grpo_v2/README.md:L184`).
  - Compute: no GPU-h is given for the recipe.
    - "The L3 stages of seeds 1 and 2, with their screening reads, ran in parallel from 7:52 to 10:23 am on serrano…
      about 2.5 h" (`RECIPE.md:L213-214`).
    - "GRPO runs about 1.9 min per iteration here (about 3 on serrano)" (`LM:TT/EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md:L152`), on RTX 3090s.
    - My arithmetic, not reported: L2 is about 39 × 1.9 ≈ 74 min per seed.
- **EI, the baseline.** "one k-32 round per stage, from the same S1g3 seeds" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L246`), which is 5,000 ×
  32 attempts followed by one 600-step fine-tune (`LM:TT/grpo_v2/README.md:L479-480`).
- **Seeds.** Training seeds 0, 1, 2. The RL seed equals the Stage-1 seed (`--seed $S`). The eval sampling seed is 0 for
  every read (`line.sh:L304`).
- **Reviewed?** No review of the recipe numbers is stated anywhere I read. The documents are `written_by: agent:claude`
  (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L3`), and the tables are generated ("do not edit by hand", `LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L1`). The
  `385691b` commit "GRPO v2 review fixes" names no reviewer.

## 4. Read-out protocol

### 4.1 Pipeline (who computes what)

1. **`line.sh`.** After L3 it calls `read1 SET FILES K READARM [MAXSTEPS [ARMNAME CKPT [MAXREJ]]]`
   (`LM:TT/grpo_v2/launch/line.sh:L300`), which runs
   `code.thinking.eval --ckpt $cp --sets $2 --out $o --k $3 --budgets $RB --arms $4 --batch $BATCH --seed 0
   --temperature 0.8 --max_action 512 --max_steps $ms --max_rej $mr` (L303-304). The defaults are `ms=96`, `mr=10` and
   the stage's L3 checkpoint (L301).
   - Full reads: "for ra in plain logical" on t1320, tb72 and charles173 (L346-349). Plain only on hand14 (L355) and
     t2130 (L356).
   - Knob pilots read only "t1320 … t2130 … charles173 … plain", because "knob selection never sees tb72" (L340-343).
2. **`eval.py`.**
   - Each problem's prompt is repeated k times: `prompts = [r["prompt"] for r in recs for _ in range(a.k)]`
     (`LM:code/thinking/eval.py:L268`).
   - The sampler is `think_generate(...)` (L292-316), then `res = judge(tok, prompts, res, a.lean)` (L318).
   - Each row stores `k=k, n_ok=sum(bool(x["ok"]) for x in att), ok=[...], status=[...]` (L129-132).
3. **`v2_results.py`.**
   - It reads `eval/v2/<set>_<arm>_s<seed>_<readarm>_k<k>/rows_<readarm>_B64.jsonl` (`LM:ME/v2_results.py:L373`).
   - It restricts rows to the subsets (L349-366) and computes `stats.ci` and `stats.paired_diff` for `KS = (1, 5)`
     (L646, L437-459).
   - `figures._thm_from_row` sets c = `n_ok`, n = `k` (`LM:code/thinking/figures.py:L171-173`).

### 4.3 Plain vs guided reads vs the training sampler

- **Plain read** (headline).
  - "`plain` ends an attempt at the first rejected action" (`LM:code/thinking/sample.py:L33`).
  - The causes are an environment rejection (`"S:" + why`), a truncated action, or a context overflow (L869-878).
    The code is `if arm == "plain" or … >= max_rej: … finish(a, "fail:" + (cause if arm == "plain" else "max_rej"))`
    (L916, L929).
  - `step_check` only flags in the plain arm: `if lcause and arm == "logical": cause = "L:" + lcause` (L890-891).
  - There are no retries and no verifier feedback; Lean judges the finished proof.
- **Guided read** (`--arms logical --max_rej 10`).
  - "`logical` also redraws one `step_check` rejects. **A redraw is the whole think + action**" (`sample.py:L34-35`).
  - Redraws are without replacement over the decoded segment (L35-38).
  - The counter `a.rej += 1` (L944) is never reset when a step is accepted. Acceptance resets only `forbid`,
    `rejected_actions`, `fb` and `step_rej` (L987-990). So the cap is 10 redraws **per attempt**, and the 11th rejection
    ends the attempt. Leon's §8 note agrees: "the guided read redraws blind, at most 10 rejected steps per attempt"
    (`LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L1230-1231`).
- **Soundness of `step_check`.**
  - "0 false rejects in 314,541 (state, step) pairs, on Lean 4.34.1" (Dan's validation).
  - "166 of 166 checker rejects were rejected by Lean itself" on Leon's Lean 4.34.0 (`LM:TT/grpo_v2/README.md:L30-32`).
- **Training sampler** (not used in reads).
  - The logical arm with `--max_rej 0`: "The first step that the environment or `step_check` rejects ends the attempt,
    and that draw gets a step penalty of 0.1" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L18-19`).
  - Training is therefore stricter than the plain read (step_check-invalid steps also end the attempt) and runs at a
    hotter temperature (1.0).

### 4.4 Judge (who decides "correct")

- **Lean alone.**
  - `judge`: "Lean on every finished attempt's literal text; sets res[i]['ok'] and res[i]['nd']"
    (`LM:code/thinking/sample.py:L1017`).
  - It calls `L.gate(...)` (L1019-1025) and sets `res[i]["ok"] = not nd.startswith("LEAN")` (L1029).
  - `lean.gate` wraps the fork's `lean_gate.gate` and only sets the binary (`LM:code/thinking/lean.py:L33-46`). The
    binary is "~/.elan/bin/lean (4.34.0 here; pods run 4.34.1)" (L4-5). "~/.elan is on NFS (Lean 4.34.0, default
    toolchain), and the gate runs core Lean … with no Mathlib" (`LM:TT/grpo_v2/launch/README.md:L221-223`).
- **No `nd_verify`.**
  - "**Lean alone decides** (Dan, 2026-09-27…). This file no longer calls `nd_verify`."
    (`LM:code/thinking/vendor/danstate/lean_gate.py:L7-8`).
  - "`nd_verify` judges nothing any more: not the in-loop reward, not evaluation, not coverage, not counting"
    (`…/vendor/danstate/lean_judge.py:L4`).
  - `git grep nd_verify` over `LM:code/thinking/` finds only the vendoring loader and vendored files; there is no call in
    eval, grpo or ei.
- **Acceptance rule.**
  - Each theorem's text goes into a chunk file run with `lean -DmaxErrors=100000000` (`lean_gate.py:L75`).
  - A theorem is accepted iff no `…:<line>:<col>: error` line falls in its line range: regex at L58,
    `return [k not in bad for k in range(len(srcs))]` at L96.
  - A crashed or timed-out chunk is split recursively, and a single crashing theorem is rejected (L89-95).
  - The reject-only prefilter is on by default: `PREFILTER = os.environ.get('LEAN_PREFILTER', 'on')` (L55).
  - A rejected text is returned as `'LEANREJ ' + nd`, "parsed the grammar, rejected by the pre-filter or by Lean: never
    counted" (L270).
  - Warnings are not errors. `git grep -i sorry` on the Lean tokenizer and prefilter finds nothing, so the grammar
    appears unable to emit `sorry` (an inference; I did not check further).
- **The RL reward uses the same judge.** `from .sample import … judge` (`LM:code/thinking/grpo.py:L305`),
  `res = judge(tok, prompts, res, a.lean)` (L1289), and "Reward. 1 if Lean accepts the whole proof, else 0" (L17).
- **Earlier Leon work used a different judge.** The ndbench benchmarking of 2026-10-01 used "**Judge**: `nd_v2` =
  vendored `nd_verify` plus a forward-citation check" (`LB:experiment-summaries/2026-09-30-benchmarking/README.md:L28`).
  Those numbers are on other (whole-proof) models and are not comparable. `LV` documents the circular-proof bug in
  upstream `nd_verify` and says "Lean-judged results are unaffected" (`LV:code/shared_code/nd_verify/README.md:L32`).

### 4.5 Failures and truncations

- Every attempt counts in n, with no exclusion: `n = int(r.get("k") or …)` (`LM:code/thinking/figures.py:L173`), and
  `k=k` is written per row (`eval.py:L129`).
- Any status other than `done` gives `nd = … "LEANPARSE env: " + status` and `text=None`
  (`LM:code/thinking/sample.py:L673, L693`).
  - The attempt is then never sent to Lean, and `ok` stays False (L1026-1027).
  - These statuses are `fail:S:<env reason>`, `fail:truncated` (action > 512 tokens), `fail:context` (root/memo formats
    only), `fail:step_cap` (96 steps) and, in guided reads, `fail:max_rej`.
- A `done` attempt that Lean (or the prefilter) rejects counts as a failure.
- Problems that load but are never solved count as 0. `load_sets` drops nothing
  (`…/vendor/danstate/guided_eval.py:L24-33`).

### 4.6 Estimator and intervals

- **Per problem.** "unbiased estimate 1 - C(n-c, k) / C(n, k) (Chen et al. 2021) from n attempts with c accepted"
  (`LM:code/thinking/stats.py:L11-12`). The code is `1 - prod_{i<k} (n - c - i) / (n - i)` (L134-142). It matches
  `eval.pass_at_k` (`LM:code/thinking/eval.py:L77-81`). pass@1 is therefore c/n, and pass@5 on 21–30 comes from n = 8.
- **Aggregate.** "the mean over seeds and theorems" (`stats.py:L10-11`), as `float(V.mean())` (L470). The value is "% of
  the set" (`LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L3`). It is not any-of-n coverage; `solved` exists in summary.json but is not
  used in the v2 tables.
- **Intervals.**
  - "Every draw resamples seeds with replacement… and theorems with replacement, with one theorem resample shared by
    every arm and seed" (`stats.py:L14-16`). `DRAWS = 4000`, `LEVEL = 0.95` (L63-64). Percentile intervals.
  - Differences come from `stats.paired_diff` "on a shared problem resample, with seeds paired when both arms have the
    same Stage-1 seeds" (`LM:ME/v2_results.py:L19-20`).
  - Caveat in the code: "With S = 3 the seed level has 10 distinct resamples, and the bootstrap variance of a mean
    understates the seed variance by (S - 1) / S" (`stats.py:L24-25`).
  - Single-seed pilot intervals "cover problem and sampling noise but not run-to-run training noise"
    (`LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L1120-1122`).
- **Matched-token reads** (row 18). k* = X / (mean sampled tokens per attempt), interpolated linearly in log k. Sampled
  tokens are think + `</think>` + action + rejected redraws, with prefill excluded (`stats.py:L30-36`).

## 5. Suspicious items

1. **textbook72 is a design set, and RECIPE does not say so.**
   - RECIPE reports textbook72 74.0 / 82.3 as observed and uses it in comparisons ("ahead of EI (+5.6)"). The RL pools
     come from a generator designed from dev58 failures (§4.8).
   - `git grep -i 'design\|dev58\|gap anal'` on `RECIPE.md` and on the summary README finds nothing; the only hits are
     unrelated "sealed" labels.
   - THINKING_UPLIFT states the caveat for the older v1 models: "textbook72 is a design set for them, not a clean test"
     (`LM:TT/THINKING_UPLIFT.md:L200-202`). The v2 pools come from the same generator family.
2. **"textbook72 stays sealed" (Leon, 2026-10-16) vs how it was used.**
   - The decision: `LT:TT/EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md:L20`.
   - textbook72 was read for every full arm, and for the five component pilots whose results selected the merged recipe
     (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L313-314`).
   - It is the set Leon reports the largest GRPO-over-EI advantages on, alongside Charles149.
3. **Selection sets are also headline sets.**
   - "KL 0 was chosen on the generated sets (13–20, 16–20, 21–30) and on Charles149… So the gains on them are partly
     selected" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L308-309`).
   - Leon now names Charles149 "the source proxy" for future selection (`LT:TT/EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md:L20`).
   - No untouched, textbook-like test set remains in this line of work.
4. **The noise band is understated, and the tuning gains are near noise.**
   - RECIPE: "The run-to-run noise band from the "nothing changed" rerun is about 1–1.5 points"
     (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L298-299`). That comes from the seed-0 knob rerun ("-1.2 [-2.3, -0.1]",
     `LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L1132`).
   - The seed-2 L3 rerun with nothing changed moved 13–20 pass@1 by "+1.3 [+0.2, +2.4]" (L877) and Charles149 by
     "+2.9 [-0.1, +6.0] | +3.7 [+1.2, +6.4]" (L883).
   - Single-seed problem-level intervals therefore exclude 0 for pure reruns.
   - The merged components' seed-2 gains (+2.1 to +3.9, L827-857) sit within about 1–2.6 points of that rerun. The
     merged recipe is not resolved at 3 seeds ("+1.2 [-2.4, +4.0]", L335, with seed 1 at "-2.6", L333).
5. **The headline arm is not clearly above the starting arm across "worlds".**
   - Serrano's v2-start arm (GRPO + verifier (stop), seeds 0 and 1) read 33.0 / 57.0 on 13–20 and 75.5 / 85.8 on
     textbook72 (`LM:ME/v2_results.md:L15`, 2026-10-11).
   - The same arm in the ynez world reads 29.2 / 53.0 and 74.8 / 81.7 (`LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L15`).
   - The final recipe (32.1 / 57.8, L24) is below serrano's v2 start on 13–20 pass@1. The worlds are not pooled by
     design (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L219-220`), but the gap shows Stage-1 and pool rebuild variation of about 4 points, larger
     than the recipe's KL-0 gain.
6. **Training pools and protocol choices were shaped by test-set inspection.**
   - The 3-premise rewrites were added because "13–20 is 78% 3-premise" (`LM:TT/EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md:L236-237`) and
     "98% of the 21–30 set has 3+ premises; the main pools 0%" (L77).
   - The read budget B64 was chosen from 13–20 reads ("B64 is the thinking model's best budget per attempt",
     `LM:TT/THINKING_UPLIFT.md:L117-122`).
   - v1 headline reads were at B256, "fixed before any eval" (`LM:TT/RL_RESULTS.md:L48-50`). The protocol changed
     between v1 and v2.
7. **The method choice reversed, with unequal tuning.**
   - 2026-10-08: "**Decision:** EI is the RL method" (`LM:TT/OVERNIGHT_20261008.md:L188`).
   - 2026-10-16: "GRPO is the RL method from now on, and expert iteration (EI) is retired" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L10`).
   - At the untuned v2 start, GRPO + verifier (stop) was behind EI + verifier on 13–20 ("-1.9 [-3.7, -0.1] |
     -4.4 [-6.9, -1.9]", `LM:ME/v2_results_ynez.md:L750`). Plain GRPO was further behind ("-3.2 [-5.3, -1.1] |
     -7.4 [-11.8, -3.4]", L670).
   - After about 15 GRPO pilots, against about 5 EI pilots (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L318-322`), the recipe is only level with
     EI on the generated sets.
   - EI was retired before its pilots' reads were analysed ("reads on disk, not analysed since EI is retired",
     `LT:TT/EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md:L34-35`).
8. **S1g3 baseline numbers lack a protocol.**
   - "textbook72 pass@1 is 43.8 / 39.6 / 32.3%" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L71-72`) gives no k, budget or temperature. Neither
     does its source (`LM:TT/EXPERIMENT_QUEUE.md:L139-142`).
   - Serrano's "40.7 / 43.0" average 41.85, which matches OVERNIGHT's B256 two-seed 41.8 (`LM:TT/OVERNIGHT_20261008.md:L174`).
     So these were probably B256 reads, while the RL reads are at B64. My inference.
   - The jump from Stage 1 (≈38.6 mean) to RL (74.0) therefore mixes RL and read budget.
   - The retrained ynez S1g3 seeds differ from serrano's by up to 3.4 points (43.0 vs 39.6 for seed 1).
9. **The KL-0 gain is not robust to the read arm.**
   - Under guided reads it is level, and negative on Charles149 at pass@5 ("−2.6 [−4.9, −0.6]",
     `LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L286`). RECIPE states this.
   - The "Observed" summary table reports plain reads only (L31-39).
10. **Seed 1's final checkpoint is not a 39-iteration model.** Its weights are "it 30 plus one iteration at lr × 0.25"
    after two think-ban rollbacks (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L333-334`). Seed 0 rolled back once (L332). The 3-seed means pool
    unequal training.
11. **Terminology hazard: "k".** RECIPE's set table "| set | items | k |" (`LM:TT/RECIPE.md:L231`) uses k for attempts per
    problem (16 / 16 / 8 / 64 / 64), while the metrics are pass@1 and pass@5. A reader could take "k 64" for pass@64.
12. **Bootstrap with 3 seeds is optimistic at the seed level.** "understates the seed variance by (S - 1) / S"

    arms. That suits paired differences but means no read was redrawn with an independent eval seed.
