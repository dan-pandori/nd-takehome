In zoological contexts, this concept is vital for maintaining breed standards among mammals such as canines or felines, where specific physical and behavioral attributes are preserved through structured breeding programs. Kennel clubs and agricultural registries meticulously document pedigrees to uphold these ideals. However, applying this terminology to humans provokes significant ethical debates.
