## Prompt (sent to the model verbatim)

Prove the product of two primitive polynomials in a UFD R[x] is primitive. Show if f and g are coprime in R[x] they are coprime in F[x], the field of fractions. The response must start with a verb.

## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 79 words)

Let f and g be primitive polynomials in R[x]. Suppose their product fg is not primitive, so a nonunit d divides all coefficients. Factoring d into primes p₁,…,pₙ, pick any p dividing d. Modulo p, R/pR is a domain, so f and g are nonzero but fg≡0, contradiction. Thus fg is primitive. If f and g are coprime in R[x], then ∃a,b ∈ R[x] with af + bg=1, which in F[x] shows gcd(f,g)=1, so they are coprime in F[x].
