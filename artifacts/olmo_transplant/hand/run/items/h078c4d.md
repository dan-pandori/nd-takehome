## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 203 words)

* The function \( f \) is Riemann integrable on \([0, 5]\).  
  - It is bounded since its values are restricted to \(\{1, 2, 3, 4\}\), and it has only jump discontinuities at the points \( x = 1, 2, \) and \( 3 \), which are isolated and hence form a set of Lebesgue measure zero. This satisfies the criteria for Riemann integrability (boundedness and discontinuities on a measure-zero set).

* The integral of \( f \) over \([0, 5]\) is equal to \( 14 \).  
  - The function \( f \) is piecewise constant over four intervals; the integral is computed by summing the areas of rectangles over each interval:  
    -- On \([0, 1]\), \( f(x) = 1 \). Area = \( 1 \times 1 = 1 \).  
    -- On \( (1, 2] \), \( f(x) = 3 \). Area = \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \).  
    -- On \( (2, 3) \), \( f(x) = 2 \). Area = \( 2 \times 1 = 2 \).  
    -- On \([3, 5]\), \( f(x) = 4 \). Area = \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \).  
  - Summing these areas: \( 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14 \).
