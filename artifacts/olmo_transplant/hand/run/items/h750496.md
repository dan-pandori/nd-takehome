  - Discontinuities occur at isolated points, which do not affect Riemann integrability since the set of discontinuities has measure zero  
* The function f is bounded on [0, 5], as its range is {1, 2, 3, 4}  
  - All values of f lie between 1 and 4, ensuring the existence of upper and lower bounds  
* The Riemann integral can be computed by summing the areas of rectangles under f over each subinterval  
  - [0, 1]: f(x) = 1, area = 1 × (1 - 0) = 1  
  - (1, 2]: f(x) = 3, area = 3 × (2 - 1) = 3  
  - (2, 3): f(x) = 2, area = 2 × (3 - 2) = 2  
  - [3, 5]: f(x) = 4, area = 4 × (5 - 3) = 8  
* Total integral = 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14  
  - Discontinuities at individual points do not contribute to the integral, as their width is zero
