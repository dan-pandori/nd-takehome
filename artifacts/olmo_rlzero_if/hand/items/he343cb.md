Shinto shrines are sacred spaces in Japanese culture dedicated to various kami (spirits, deities, or natural forces). These structures are often located near rivers, forests, or mountains, symbolizing the connection between the natural world and the spiritual realm.

4. **Hatanuki (purification baths)**
