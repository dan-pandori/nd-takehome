Suppose a sequence of polynomials B_n(x) converge pointwise to f(x) on [0, 1]. Does it follow that the B_n converge uniformly? Ensure that stop words constitute no more than 20% of the total words in your response.

## Answer

No Pointwise convergence polynomials on [0,1] need not imply uniform convergence Example B_n(x)=x^n converges pointwise to discontinuous f Uniform convergence requires continuous limit Counterexample exists
