## Answer

In a world where feudalism’s grip never loosened, castles of stone and steel dominated every horizon.  
  Dynasties ruled through aeons, their lineage enforced by technologies that read loyalty in DNA and punish dissent with neural shackles.  
    The Renaissance was erased from history books, its art burned as heretical, its philosophies deemed treasonous to the natural order.  
      Cities withered into fortified company towns, their citizens serfs bound to corporate-lords who owned the air they breathed.  
        The Industrial Revolution spawned neither capitalism nor democracy, but a global network of automated factories guarded by mercenary-knights in powered armor.  
          World Wars were replaced by eternal border skirmishes between fiefdoms, fought with plasma rifles and alliances forged over blood-oaths older than nations.  
            Now, interstellar empires sprawl across galaxies, with noble houses claiming entire worlds as their ancestral domains, their ships emblazoned with crests older than time itself.
