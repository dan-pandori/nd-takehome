## Answer

In lines of stone, truth takes form,  
Image clear, not masked or marred.  
Metaphor unforced, sharp and true,  
Rhyme tunes the ear, rhythm too.  

Theme bold stands unchained and bare,  
Sound bends the mind, not air.  
Each verse holds depth, unpeeled,  
Impact felt, not sealed.  

Structure serves the heart’s intent,  
Flow smooth as paths are bent.  
No forced lines break the tread,  
Poet’s voice, alive and spread.  

Marks given for effort held fast,  
Art that time cannot outcast.
