## Answer

Form holds tight, rhythm true,  
Images bloom, colors leap,  
Heart speaks clear, depths sincere,  
Voice unique, path unseen.  

Theme resounds, message bold,  
Rhyme’s soft chime, meter’s pulse,  
In lines that stir, transform, unfold—
