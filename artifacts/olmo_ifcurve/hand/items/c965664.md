## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay of a mental asylum doctor ordering a schizophrenic patient to take his pills, the patient refuses, and makes him take the pills to prove they are safe. The doctor obliges, but when he opens his eyes, the patient is gone, a figment of his imagination, revealing it was the doctor that was schizophrenic the whole time. ## Answer

A dimly lit asylum office hums with the drone of flickering fluorescent lights. 🌑  

Dr. Ellis, a man in his late forties with streaks of gray in his hair, adjusts his glasses nervously. 👓  

Across from him sits Raymond, a gaunt figure in a worn hospital gown, staring blankly ahead. 😔  

“You need to take your medication, Raymond,” Dr. Ellis says firmly, tapping a pill bottle on the desk. 💊  

Raymond’s eyes snap open, his voice a rasp. 😈  

“No… you take them first.” 🤯  

“But that’s against protocol—” Dr. Ellis protests weakly. 🤔  

Raymond’s smile widens, eerie and unsettling. 😈  

With trembling hands, Dr. Ellis fumbles with the blister pack. 🖐️  

He pops the pill into his mouth and swallows dryly. 💊  

The room spins as the medication kicks in. 🌀  

When he blinks, Raymond is gone in a swirl of static. 🌀  

Only an empty chair remains. 🪑  

Dr. Ellis grips the desk, his own reflection mocking him in the glass. 🪞  

The camera zooms in on his widening pupils. 👀  

“I’m the one who’s broken,” he whispers. 😔  

The screen fades to black. 🖤  

Distant whispers fill the void. 🗣️
