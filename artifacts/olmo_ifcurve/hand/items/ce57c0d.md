## Answer

* Reduced sunlight during eclipses disrupts photosynthesis  
  - This leads to a temporary decrease in chlorophyll synthesis and ATP production, limiting energy availability for growth and metabolic processes  
* Plants exhibit short-term stress responses to sudden light changes  
  - Stomata may close to conserve water, reducing CO2 intake and temporarily slowing carbon fixation  
* Transient cooling during eclipses can affect enzyme activity  
  - Lower temperatures might slow biochemical reactions involved in photosynthesis, though effects are minimal due to the eclipse’s short duration
