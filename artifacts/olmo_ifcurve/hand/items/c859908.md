# Item c859908

## Prompt (as the model received it)

A spring pendulum with mass m on a spring with stiffness k, performing undamped harmonic oscillations, has at the initial moment of time t = 0 the coordinate x = x0 and velocity V =V0. What will be the amplitude and initial phase of these oscillations? ## Answer

* Amplitude of the oscillations:  
  - \( A = \sqrt{x_0^2 + \dfrac{m V_0^2}{k}} \)  
  - Derived from energy considerations, where \( A \) combines the initial kinetic and potential energies in the system.  

* Initial phase of the oscillations:  
  - \( \phi = \arctan\left( -\dfrac{V_0 \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}}{x_0} \right) \)  
  - Determined using the ratio of initial velocity to displacement, accounting for the system’s spring constant \( k \), mass \( m \), and the need to resolve the phase quadrant appropriately.
