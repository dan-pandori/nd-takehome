### **Explanation:**  
This song uses nesting brackets **( )**, **[ ]**, and **{ }** to symbolize the *layered incomprehensibility* of infinity. Each bracket level represents a deeper dive into existential abstraction:  

1. **Parentheses ( )**: The observable universe—stars, galaxies, the laws of physics we *think* we understand.  
2. **Brackets [ ]**: Subatomic realms (quantum foam, particles that defy intuition).  
3. **Braces { }**: Theoretical physics (multiverses, string theory, concepts beyond math’s reach).  
4. **Nested depth**: The bridge and outro push further—into *meta*-infinity (paradoxes, self-referential chaos, and the limits of language/math itself).  

**What We Cannot Understand:**  
- **Boundlessness**: Infinity has no "borders"—no matter how deep we nest brackets (or equations), there’s always another layer (e.g., Hilbert’s infinite hotel with vacant rooms for new guests *forever*).  
- **Paradoxes**: Concepts like "infinite regression" or "actual infinity" collapse human cognition. The lyrics hint at this with crumbling math and swallowed echoes.  
- **nemonic Limits**: Our brains evolved for finite survival. We can *graph* infinity (e.g., 1⁄∞ = 0), but experienced "forever" remains an alien concept.  
- **The Void’s Silence**: The chorus’ " Brisbane //\"There are no edges we cannot bracket" underscores that even language—or any symbolic system—fails to *contain* the infinite.  

Like the lyrics, the universe may be an infinite regress: a nesting of questions we can *pose*, but never fully *answer*. The song mirrors this tension—celebrating wonder while drowning in its vast, un graspable scale.
