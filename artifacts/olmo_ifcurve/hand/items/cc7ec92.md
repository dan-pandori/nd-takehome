## Answer

Deism's assertion that God is a non-intervening creator fails to account for personal experiences of divine interaction, such as answered prayers, which imply an active God. Furthermore, the explanatory power of natural laws suggests the universe operates independently, making a detached deity superfluous.  
*Empirical claims of religious experiences undermine the idea of a disengaged creator.  
*The self-sustaining nature of physical and biological systems reduces reliance on a hands-off divine architect.
