**SQL Interview Question: Identifying Top Customers with Multi-Category Purchase Constraints**

**Problem Statement:**  
You are working with a sales database containing three tables: `Customers`, `Orders`, and `OrderItems`, as well as a `Products` table for product details. Write a SQL query to identify the **top 3 customers** who meet the following criteria over the last 12 months:

1. **Only include orders where products are currently in stock.**
2. **Exclude customers who placed orders from more than two distinct product categories** during this period.
3. **Sum the total purchase amount** (calculated as `OrderItems.Quantity * Products.Price`) for each customer.
4. Rank customers by their **total spending in descending order** and return the top 3. Exclude customers with no qualifying purchases in the timeframe.

**Table Structure (Assumed):**
- **`Customers`**: `CustomerID` (PK), `Name`, `Email`, ...  
- **`Orders`**: `OrderID` (PK), `CustomerID` (FK to Customers), `OrderDate`, ...  
- **`OrderItems`**: `OrderItemID` (PK), `OrderID` (FK to Orders), `ProductID` (FK to Products), `Quantity`  
- **`Products`**: `ProductID` (PK), `CategoryID` (FK to Categories), `ProductName`, `Price`, `Stock` (_stock indicates if in stock: 1=in stock, 0=out of stock)  

**Follow-Up Questions (to assess understanding):**  
- How might you handle a situation where a customer has multiple orders for the same product but different categories?  
- What adjustments would you make if "stock" is a boolean (e.g., `in_stock` column with `TRUE/FALSE`)?  
- How would the query change if "categories" were hierarchical (e.g., nested categories like "Electronics > TVs")?

---

**Solution SQL Query:**  
```sql
WITH RecentOrders AS (
    SELECT 
        oi.OrderID,
        c.CustomerID,
        p.CategoryID,
        SUM(oi.Quantity * p.Price) AS TotalAmount,
        COUNT(DISTINCT p.CategoryID) AS CategoryCount
    FROM Customers c
    INNER JOIN Orders o ON c.CustomerID = o.CustomerID
    INNER JOIN OrderItems oi ON o.OrderID = oi.OrderID
    INNER JOIN Products p ON oi.ProductID = p.ProductID
    WHERE 
        o.OrderDate >= DATE_SUB(CURDATE(), INTERVAL 12 MONTH) -- Last 12 months
        AND p.Stock = 1 -- Only in-stock products
    GROUP BY c.CustomerID, p.CategoryID  -- Aggregate per customer and category
    HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT p.CategoryID) <= 2  -- Exclude customers with >2 categories
)
SELECT 
    CustomerID,
    SUM(TotalAmount) AS TotalSpent,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(TotalAmount) DESC) AS Rank
FROM RecentOrders
GROUP BY CustomerID
HAVING Rank <= 3
ORDER BY TotalSpent DESC;
```

---

**Explanation of the Approach:**  
1. **Common Table Expressions (CTE):**  
   - The `RecentOrders` CTE aggregates data at the **customer and category level** to compute:  
     - Total spending per category.  
     - The number of distinct categories each customer interacted with.  
   - Filters ensure only in-stock products and orders from the last year are considered.

2. **Filtering Categories:**  
   - `COUNT(DISTINCT p.CategoryID) <= 2` in the `RecentOrders` CTE *pre-aggregates* customers who exceeded the two-category limit. This avoids including invalid records early, improving performance.

3. **Final Aggregation:**  
   - The outer query sums spending across all qualifying categories per customer.  
   - `DENSE_RANK()` assigns a rank to customers by their total spending.  
   - The final `HAVING Rank <= 3` ensures only the top 3 are returned, avoiding ties (unlike `LIMIT`).  
   - `GROUP BY CustomerID` is necessary because `SUM(TotalAmount)` combines category-level totals into a customer-level value.

---

**Edge Cases Handled:**  
- **Zero Spending Customers:** Excluded via the initial `GROUP BY` in `RecentOrders` (customers with no qualifying orders are automatically omitted).  
- **Customers with Multiple Orders in Same Category:** Aggregates correctly by summing quantities/amounts within categories.  
- **CategoryID `NULL`s:** Assume `Products` ensures `CategoryID` is always populated; otherwise, include `WHERE p.CategoryID IS NOT NULL`.

**Complexity Considerations:**  
- **Indexing:** The query relies heavily on indexes on:  
  - `Orders.OrderDate` (for the time filter).  
  - `Products.Stock` (quick in-stock check).  
  - Foreign keys (`OrderItems.OrderID`, `OrderItems.ProductID`, `Orders.CustomerID`).  
- **Performance:** The `RecentOrders` CTE reduces the working set early, avoiding large intermediate results.
