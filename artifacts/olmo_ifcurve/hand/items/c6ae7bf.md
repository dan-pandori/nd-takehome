The dimly lit office of a mental asylum flickers with uncertainty. 🏥  
Dr. Elias Vorne, a tense psychiatrist, faces a smirking patient. 😳  
“Your pills,” the patient taunts, throwing the bottle. 💊  
“I demand you take them first!” Dr. Vorne commands. 👊

The room whirls—the patient is gone. 🌀  
A nurse peers in, concerned. 👩  
“Who were you arguing with, Doctor?” she asks softly. ❓
