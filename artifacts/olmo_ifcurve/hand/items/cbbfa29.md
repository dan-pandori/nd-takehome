## Answer

Ext. Nursing Home Gardens - Day  

Close On A Golf Cart Navigating Through A Sea Of Reporters. Elias Thorne, 127, Wheelchair-Bound But Smiling, Is Wheeled Forward. A Golden Plaque Around His Neck Reads: 'Record Human Lifespan: 127 Years.' Congratulations From Dignitaries And Well-Wishers. A Young Reporter Asks: 'How Do You Feel, Mr. Thorne?' He Replies: 'Grateful. The Journey Isnt Over—Just Paused.'  

Int. Elias'S Private Room - Night  

Elias Lies In Bed, Eyes Wide Open. Moonlight Filters Through Cracked Window. He Runs A Hand Over A Worn Journal. Flashback Images: A Younger Elias Reading Science Magazines, Headline: 'Quantum Immortality: Scientists Claim Consciousness Migrates To Alternate Universes Upon Death.' His Voiceover: 'Each Time I Avoided Disaster... I Realized I Must Be In The Branch Where I Survived. But Now...'  

He Sits Up, Breathing Heavily. Shapeless Figures Begin To Form In The Corners—Himself At Various Ages, Dying In Different Ways. 'You Cant Jump Realm Anymore!' He Cries, Clutching His Head. The Figures Laugh, Dissolving Into Static-Noise. 'All These Years... Were They Just Luck? Or...' His Whisper Trails Off As He Identifies A Figure In The Shadow: A Splintered Version Of Himself, Aged 128, Collapsing. 'No! Not Now—'  

He Screams, Crawling Toward The Figure. The Room Spins. His Laughter Mingles With The Dying Man'S Whimper. Suddenly—Black. The Sound Of A Monitor Beeping. Fade Out.
