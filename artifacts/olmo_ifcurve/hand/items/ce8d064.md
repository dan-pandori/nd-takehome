### Explanation  
**The Song’s Meaning**:  
This poem mirrors the *infinite* through recursively nested brackets, each layer symbolizing an unending descent into abstraction. The lyrics juxtapose humanity’s relentless curiosity ("we seek in the dark") with the futility of grasping infinity—a concept that, by definition, resists closure. "A puzzle without edges" reflects paradoxes like Hilbert’s Hotel or Zeno’s tortoise, where logic bends but never resolves.  

**What We Cannot Understand**:  
1. **The Nature of "Infinite" Itself**: Mathematically, infinity is a tool, not a physical reality. We quantify it (countable vs. uncountable), but intuitively, it defies imagination.  
2. **Cosmic Infinity**: If the universe is infinite, does it contain endless copies of ourselves? Can "space" exist *without bounds*? These ideas clash with human needs for meaning and limitation.  
3. **Subjective Infinity**: "Eternity" feels incomprehensible—time without endpoints conflicts with our lived experience of change and decay.  
4. **Paradoxes**: Infinity births contradictions (e.g., ∞ + 1 = ∞), exposing gaps between formal logic and human reasoning.  

The nested brackets visually echo this theme: no matter how deeply you dive, there’s always Another layer, another unknowable truth.
