# Item c3438b2

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay from an alternate universe where the Apollo 11 lunar module suddenly stopped working. The astronauts were doomed, and were forced to take cyanide pills and slowly lose consciousness as they listen to nixon's speech about how much they will be considered heroes. Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

## Verifiable constraints

- `format:line_indent` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

INT. LUNAR MODULE "EAGLE" - MOON SURFACE - NIGHT  

    THE CABIN SILENT. ONLY THE HUM OF STATIC EMERGES FROM ALDRIN’S HELMET.  

        A LOW BUZZ RISES FROM THE INSTRUMENTS. THEN—A SHARP CRACK.  

                THE ENGINES DIE. LIGHTS FLICKER RED.  

                    “HOUSTON, THIS IS EAGLE. WE HAVE NO THRUST. ABORTING…”  

                        MISSION CONTROL’S VOICE CRACKLES, STATIC-KISSED:  

                                “AFFIRMATIVE. INITIATE TEHF. PILL DISPENSER ACTIVATED. ADMINISTER CYANIDE…”  

                                    ALDRIN’S GLOVE GRABS THE VIAL. ARMSTRONG’S HAND TREMEOUS.  

                                        “COPY. ON THREE… TWO… ONE…”  

                                            THE CAPSULES DISAPPEAR DOWN THEIR THROATS.  

                                                A TINNY SPEAKER BLARES A FAMILIAR VOICE:  

                                                        “FELLOW AMERICANS… TODAY WE MOURN NOT A DEFEAT—BUT A TRIUMPH OF COURAGE…”  

                                                            ARMSTRONG’S HEAD DROOPS AGAINST THE CONTROLS.  

                                                                “EARTH… GOODNIGHT…”  

                                                                    ALDRIN’S EYES FLUTTER CLOSED.  

                                                                        THE MODULE drifts in silent moonlight.  

                                                                            HOUSTON’S CONSOLES DARKEN. THE WORD “LOST” BLINKS CRIMSON.
