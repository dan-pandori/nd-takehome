## Answer

Observe the soul the verse conveys—  
Check images that bloom within each line,  
Structure stands firm, each rhyme a steady breath,  
Heart's cry must ring clear, not muffled by the page,  
Original thoughts, unshaped by common phrase,  
Movement should glide like rivers carving stone,  
Theme shines bright, not lost in fog or shade,  
Score high the blend as art and truth engage,  
Thus judge the song as feeling finds its page.
