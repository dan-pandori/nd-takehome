### **Explanation**  
The song uses nested parentheses `()`, brackets `[]`, and braces `{}` to *visually* symbolize the infinite layers of existence—a metaphoric nesting doll of mysteries. Each bracket type represents a "layer" of complexity:  

1. **Parentheses `()`**: The universal questions (e.g., *"Why does anything exist?"*).  
2. **Brackets `[]`**: The frameworks of physics and time that humans build to understand the universe.  
3. **Braces `{}`**: The enigmatic, existential "whys" we cannot assign logic to (dark matter, consciousness, free will).  

The nesting **deepens** across verses and choruses, hitting **five levels** in the climax:  
- `( ((( [[] [[] [[] [[] ]] ]]] ) ) )` (e.g., lines like *"(){[[{...}]]}...*").  

### **What We Cannot Understand**  
- **The Unseen**: The infinite isn’t a *number* to calculate—it’s a **concept resisting containment**, like the unresolved parentheses in the bridge and outro.  
- **Paradox as Truth**: The chorus’s "infinite mirage" reflects Zeno’s paradoxes or the idea that even if you could "grasp" infinity, it would require infinite steps—impossible for finite minds.  
- **Cosmic Irony**: The bridge mocks human hubris; even "dots" of knowledge are illusory. The outro’s final unclosed brackets (`(...][[[[[`) symbolize futility: the infinite cannot be neatly encapsulated or "known."  

The song’s fractured rhythm mirrors our incomplete grasp— fragments like *"binary stars"* nod to binary logic’s limits, while *"math breaks at its core"* hints at Gödel’s incompleteness theorems. In the end, the infinite remains **eloquently silent**.
