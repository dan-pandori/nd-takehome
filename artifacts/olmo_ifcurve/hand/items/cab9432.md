The Oldest Man In The World  

Int. Grand Convention Hall - Day  

Elias Thorne, 127, Pale And Frail, Is Wheeled To The Podium Amid Thunderous Applause. A Crowd Of Reporters And Scientists Hold Up Signs Reading "Longest Human Life Ever Recorded." A Child In The Front Row Wipes Tears, Her Hand Raised.

Emotional

Cut To:  

Int. Elias's Bedroom - Night  

The Room Is Dimly Lit By A Single Lamp. Framed Photos Cover The Walls, One Showing A Young Elias Standing Beside A Burning Car. He Lies In Bed, Eyes Open, Staring At The Ceiling. His Breath Is Shallow, Raspy.  

He Mutters To Himself, Voice Trembling.  

Quantum Immortality... The Idea That Your Consciousness Never Truly Dies, That You Keep Shifting To A Reality Where You Survive... But What If I Am,  

(His Voice Breaks)  

...Trapped In The Branch Where I Am Dying?  

He Grabs The Bedsheets, His Breathing Trembling More Violently.  

The Camera Zooms In On His Terrified Face As The Screen Fades To Black.
