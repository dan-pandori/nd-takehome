## Answer

FADE IN: THE MOON - JULY 20, 1969. A desolate gray landscape stretches endlessly, craters like scars across its face.  
  THE LUNAR MODULE, EAGLE, lies motionless, its landing gear splayed like a dying spider.  
    Inside, NEIL ARMSTRONG and BUZZ ALDRIN exchange a glance of terror.  
      ALDRIN'S fingers tremble over the dead controls. No response.  
        ARMSTRONG'S voice, heavy with finality: "We're not getting out of here."  
          MISSION CONTROL'S static-filled voice: "EAGLE, DO YOU READ? REPEAT..."  
            A long, agonizing silence. Then, suddenly —  
              NIXON'S aged voice crackles through: "THIS IS NIXON. TAKE THE PILLS. YOU WILL BE REMEMBERED."  
                The two astronauts clutch their cyanide capsules. ARMSTRONG nods. ALDRIN whispers, "Together?"  
                  They break the seals. Bitterness floods their mouths. The cabin fills with a metallic sigh as systems fail.  
                    NIXON'S words echo, now faint: "...never be forgotten..."  
                      Their eyelids grow heavy. Shadows pool around them, swallowing light.  
                        FADE TO BLACK.
