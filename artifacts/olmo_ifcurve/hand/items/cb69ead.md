The golden afternoon sun spills through the kitchen window, casting honeyed light over scattered textbooks and half-empty soda cans.

              Before you can finish, she claps a hand over your mouth (now much smaller) and giggles, already pulling out a rattlesnake-patterned pacifier. “Night-night, baby. Time for *nursery rhymes*.”
