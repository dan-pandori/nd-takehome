# Item ce59205

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay of a mental asylum doctor ordering a schizophrenic patient to take his pills, the patient refuses, and makes him take the pills to prove they are safe. The doctor obliges, but when he opens his eyes, the patient is gone, a figment of his imagination, revealing it was the doctor that was schizophrenic the whole time.  Please use an emoji at the end of every sentence.

## Answer

Dr. Elias Thorne stands in the dimly lit corridor 🌑  
He clutches a pill bottle in his trembling hand 🤚  
Patient X sits calmly on the cold floor 🧊  
Patient X mutters “I don’t need those pills” 🗣️  
Thorne demands “Take them now” 👮♂️  
Patient X gazes up with hollow eyes 👁️  
Patient X challenges “Prove they’re harmless—take one yourself” 🧪  
Thorne hesitates sweat beading his brow 💦  
He tips a pill into his mouth 💊  
He swallows it down gagging 🤢  
The room spins wildly out of control 🌀  
When he opens his eyes Patient X has vanished 👻  
His reflection reveals a frayed straitjacket 👕  
“You’re one of us” whispers the wall 👊  
Thorne screams but his voice fades 😨  
The asylum walls laugh in silence 😈
