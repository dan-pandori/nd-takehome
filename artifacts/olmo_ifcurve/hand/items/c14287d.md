# Item c14287d

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay from an alternate universe where the Apollo 11 lunar module suddenly stopped working. The astronauts were doomed, and were forced to take cyanide pills and slowly lose consciousness as they listen to nixon's speech about how much they will be considered heroes. Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

## Verifiable constraints

- `format:line_indent` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

INT. LUNAR MODULE - NIGHT  

  THE MODULE SHUDDERS ONCE—THEN SILENCE.  

    ARMSTRONG’S GLOVED HAND TREMBLES OVER DEAD CONTROLS.  

      “HOUSTON, WE’VE GOT… AN ANOMALY,” HE RASPS.  

          ALDRIN GLANCES AT EARTH, A FRAGILE BLUE MARBLE SHRINKING BELOW.  

              “NO SIGNAL. LIFE SUPPORT… FAILING.”  

                  THE EMERGENCY KIT SPRINGS OPEN—CYANIDE CAPSULES.  

                      “FOR THE JOURNEY… ONWARD,” ARMSTRONG WHISPERS.  

                          THEY SWALLOW.  

                              NIXON’S VOICE BLARES THROUGH STATIC:  

                                  “TO ALL AMERICANS, AND TO THE WORLD—THESE BRAVE MEN HAVE ETCHED THEIR NAMES INTO HISTORY! THEIR SACRIFICE WILL NOT BE FORGOTTEN… THEY ARE TRUE HEROES!”  

                                      THEIR LIPS MOVE BUT NO SOUND ESCAPES.  

                                          DARKNESS CREeps IN,  

                                              ERASING THE STARS.  

                                                  FADE TO BLACK.  

                                                      THE END.
