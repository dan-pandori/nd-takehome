## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay of a mental asylum doctor ordering a schizophrenic patient to take his pills, the patient refuses, and makes him take the pills to prove they are safe. The doctor obliges, but when he opens his eyes, the patient is gone, a figment of his imagination, revealing it was the doctor that was schizophrenic the whole time.  Please use an emoji at the end of every sentence.

## Answer

The sterile halls of Blackthorn Asylum stretch endlessly. 🕰️  
Dr. Elias Vorne adjusts his tie, fingers trembling. 👖  
A patient sits across his desk, face shrouded in shadow. 🧥  
“Take your pills,” he demands, voice fraying. 🗣️  
The patient tilts their head, a razor-thin smile forming. 😏  
“Prove they’re safe first,” they rasp, leaning forward. 👀  
Vorne’s hand hovers over the prescription bottle. 💊  
“I am your doctor,” he insists, jaw clenched. 😠  
“And I am your worst fear,” the patient whispers, eyes glowing faintly. ✨  
He snatches a pill, crushing it under his teeth. 🦷  
The room chills. ❄️  
“Drink,” the patient commands, holding out a cup of water. 💧  
He obeys, throat dry. 🤤  
The water tastes metallic. 🥶  
When he finishes, the patient is gone. 🚫  
His breath steadies… but the walls now pulse like living tissue. 🩸  
A clock ticks backward. ⏳  
“Schedule says you’re due for your dose,” a voice echoes—his own. 🔊  
Vorne staggers, glancing down at his wrist. ⌚  
A tattoo reads: PATIENT ZERO. 👒  
He screams, but no sound emerges. 😱  
The scene dissolves into a padded cell. 🛏️  
A nurse peers through the window, holding a chart titled “SCHIZOPHRENIA.” 📋  
His reflection grins back—a hollow-eyed stranger. 👺  
The screen fades to static. 📻
