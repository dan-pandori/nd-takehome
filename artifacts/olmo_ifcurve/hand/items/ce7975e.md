## Answer

Pointwise convergence of polynomials \( B_n(x) \) to \( f(x) \) on \([0,1]\) does not guarantee uniform convergence. A classic counterexample involves the sequence \( B_n(x) = nx(1 - x)^n \), which converges pointwise to \( 0 \) on \([0,1]\). However, the maximum of \( |B_n(x)| \) near \( x = 1/(n+1) \) approaches \( 1/e \), demonstrating non-vanishing sup norms. Thus, convergence lacks uniformity despite pointwise behavior, illustrating that polynomial sequences exhibit this distinction.
