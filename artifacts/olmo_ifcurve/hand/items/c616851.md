# Item c616851

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay from an alternate universe where the Apollo 11 lunar module suddenly stopped working. The astronauts were doomed, and were forced to take cyanide pills and slowly lose consciousness as they listen to nixon's speech about how much they will be considered heroes. Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

## Verifiable constraints

- `format:line_indent` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

TITLE: ECHOES OF THE MOON  

  INT. LUNAR MODULE - NIGHT  

    THE FLICKERING INSTRUMENT PANELS CAST JAGGED SHADOWS ACROSS NEIL ARMSTRONG’S FACE AS HE SLAMS A FIST AGAINST THE CONSOLE. STATIC HISSSES FROM THE COMM SYSTEM, DROWNING OUT HOUSTON’S FAINT, DESPERATE CALLS. BUZZ ALDRIN FLOATS BESIDE HIM, HIS GLOVED HAND BRUSHING A LOOSE SCREW CIRCULATING IN ZERO-G.  

      “HOUSTON… THEY’RE NOT RESPONDING,” A VOICE CRACKLES THROUGH THE STATIC, STRAINED AND OFFICIAL. “PREPARE FOR CONTINGENCY PROTOCOL.”  

        ARMSTRONG’S EYES DART TO THE LOCKER COMPARTMENT. INSIDE, TWO CYANIDE CAPSULES GLINT UNDER THE EMERGENCY LIGHTS—SILVER, LETHAL, THE FINAL TOOL IN NASA’S TOOLBOX FOR SCENARIOS LIKE THIS.  

          “WE KNEW THE RISKS,” ALDRIN SAYS, VOICE HOLLOW. HE REACHES FOR THE LOCKER LATCH.  

            NIXON’S RECORDED SPEECH BOOMS SUDDENLY THROUGH THE SPEAKER, UNRESTRAINED NOW BY MISSION CONTROL’S FILTERS:  

              “FELLOW AMERICANS, I AM HERE TO REPORT TO YOU THE DEATH OF TWO OF THE BRAVEST MEN…”  

                ARMSTRONG’S HAND TREMORS AS HE POPS ONE PILL INTO HIS MOUTH. ALDRIN FOLLOWS. THE BITTER METAL TASTE FLOODS THEIR THROATS.  

                  “THEY DID NOT RETURN, BUT THEIR SACRIFICE…” NIXON’S VOICE SWELLS, OBLIVIOUS. “…WILL LIVE FOREVER IN OUR GRATEFUL…”  

                    ARMSTRONG CLUTCHES THE PILLS, FORCING THEM DOWN. HIS VISION BLURS. ALDRIN WHISPERS SOMETHING INDISTINCT, PERHAPS A PRAYER, OR JUST AIR ESCAPING HIS LIPS.  

                      THE SPEECH CRESCENDOES, NOW A DIRGE. THE PILLS BURN. DARK EDGES CREEP INTO THEIR SIGHT.  

                        “HEROES…” NIXON’S VOICE FADES AS CONSCIOUSNESS SLIPS.  

                          THE MODULE’S HUM FADES TOO, REPLACED BY THE SILENCE OF INEVITABILITY.  

                            CREDITS ROLL OVER THE BLUE MARBLE IN THE DISTANCE, EARTH GLOWING LIKE A TOM BSTONE.
