## Answer

The equation of motion for an undamped spring pendulum is \( m \ddot{y} + k y = 0 \), derived from balancing inertial and spring forces. Incorporating viscous damping yields the equation \( m \ddot{y} + c \dot{y} + k y = 0 \).  

* The undamped equation \( m \ddot{y} + k y = 0 \) describes perpetual oscillations at a constant frequency with no energy loss.  
* The damped equation \( m \ddot{y} + c \dot{y} + k y = 0 \) models oscillations with exponentially decreasing amplitude due to energy dissipation from damping.
