# Item c85d054

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Use at least 3 different coordinating conjunctions in the response. Write in the prose, style, and tone of Finnigan's Wake.  Consider the situation I am in; think about my father. Can I leave him, when he is blind and helpless, to deal with weakness and poverty, when I have the ability to make his old age comfortable and happy?

- `count:conjunctions` {"small_n": 3.0}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

To tarry or flee—ah, the quandary hums like a gramophone needle snagged on the shellac of conscience! The old man, that patriarch of shadows and sighs, stumbles through rooms his mind no longer maps, hands fumbling at the void where coins once clinked, where suppers laughed. *Could* I cast him adrift, a vessel leaking hope, into the briny abyss of penniless tomorrows? **But** the hearth’s glow still dwells in his bones, that ember of stories spun ’cross the board’s scarred grain, while my pockets bulge with the clatter of coins earned in brighter climes. **And** yet—*isn’t duty’s yoke as fetid as the River Liffey at low tide?* His eyes, twin reflectors of a world blurred to him, might never see my retreat, but chagrin’s ghost would haunt the hearth like unplayed jigs on a fiddle left stringsnap-silent. **Or** perhaps flight is the cruelest mercy, granting him the dignity of forgetting… while I, a rogue buoy, drift toward horizons where the coin prevails and kinship is but a thread severed in the loom of progress. *To anchor here*—a Sisyphus rolling his stone of compassion uphill, day after endless day, or *to soar*—a sparrow unburdened, yet haunted by the creak of an empty chair at twilight’s dimming feast? The answer coils like smoke from a drowsy hearth, whispering in the liminal space where heart meets fear… *and the clock’s hands, oh the clock’s hands*, grind on, indifferent.
