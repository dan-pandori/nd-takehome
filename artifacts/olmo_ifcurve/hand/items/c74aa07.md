Let f / [0, 5] -> \mathbb{R} be defined by  f(x):= 1 if * 0 <= x <= 1; 3 if * 1 < x <= 2; 2 if * 2 < x < 3; 4 if * 3 <= x <= 5  Show that f is Riemann Integrable and integrate f = 14 df from 0 to 5 . ## Answer

* The function \( f \) is Riemann Integrable on \([0, 5]\).  
  - It is a step function with discontinuities only at \( x = 1 \), \( 2 \), and \( 3 \), which are measure zero sets, ensuring integrability.  

* The integral of \( f \) from \( 0 \) to \( 5 \) is calculated as follows:  
  - The interval \([0, 1]\) contributes an area of \( 1 \times 1 = 1 \).  
  - The interval \((1, 2]\) contributes an area of \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \).  
  - The interval \((2, 3)\) contributes an area of \( 2 \times 1 = 2 \).  
  - The interval \([3, 5]\) contributes an area of \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \).  
  - Summing these contributions: \( 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14 \).
