# Item c54136b

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a rubric in the form of a poem that lists several items for how to evaluate a poem. The letter w should appear less than 2 times in your response.

## Verifiable constraints

- `keywords:letter_frequency` {"let_relation": "less than", "letter": "w", "let_frequency": 2}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

A verse must breathe, not cease,  
Structure tight, cadence clear;  
Imagery bold, senses engaged;  
Original voice, not staged.  

Emotion true, ideas sparked,  
Rhyme subtle, rhythm spaced,  
Metaphors fresh, thoughts evoked,  
Coherence strong, gaps not irked.  

Poetry’s soul in each line,  
Judged by these, divine.
