## Answer

Deism's assertion that God does not intervene in the world clashes with the widespread human experience of answered prayers and religious epiphanies, which imply a responsive and engaged deity. Furthermore, the persistence of gratuitous suffering—such as preventable famines or natural disasters—directly challenges the deistic claim of a benevolent Creator who designed but abandoned the cosmos.  
* Empirical studies on prayer, like those in controlled medical settings, often report statistically significant outcomes consistent with pray-ers' expectations, defying purely naturalistic explanations.  
* The moral problem of evil intensifies under deism: if God could have designed a universe without suffering but chose not to, this raises serious questions about His moral character compared to theistic frameworks that allow for ongoing divine providence.
