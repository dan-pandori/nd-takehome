Possible causes include musculoskeletal strain (e.g., cervical tension, whiplash), temporomandibular joint dysfunction (TMJ), or anxiety-induced muscle stiffness. Throat soreness without infection may stem from gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), postnasal drip from allergies/sinus irritation, or esophageal spasm. Stress or fatigue exacerbating muscle tightness is common. Less likely but possible: reactive lymphadenopathy (benign swelling not palpable), mild cervical radiculopathy, or autoimmune conditions. Rule out trauma history or recent dental issues. Further evaluation should include assess neck mobility, ENT exam, and reflux symptoms. No red flags for emergencies like trauma or malignancy.
