**Explanation of the Song:**  
This song grapples with humanity’s struggle to grasp infinity. The imagery of "endless sky," "void," and "fraying logic" reflects how infinity defies intuitive understanding. Mathematical concepts like countable/uncountable infinities exist, yet their *existence*—a reality beyond time, space, or perception—eludes us. The chorus ("horizon’s slip") captures the futility of pinning infinity to finite frameworks; every "answer" (equation, metaphor, artwork) dissolves into paradox. The closing lines evoke awe and humility: infinity is not a thing to "solve," but a horizon forever receding, whispering truths we can’t articulate.  

**What We Cannot Understand:**  
1. **Infinite Regress:** Concepts like *eternal* time or infinite subdivisions (e.g., dividing a line infinitely) collapse under scrutiny.  
2. **Non-Intuitive Scales:** A "countably infinite" set (like integers) is "smaller" than an "uncountably infinite" continuum (like real numbers), yet both are infinite—a distinction our brains can’t truly visualize.  
3. **Transcendence:** Infinity isn’t just "very big"; it’s a *limitless direction* beyond physical laws. We experience it mathematically but can’t *feel* its weight.  

The nested brackets mirror this theme: each layer adds depth, yet the structure never truly "ends," much like infinity itself—a symbol of bounded exploration within an unbounded universe.
