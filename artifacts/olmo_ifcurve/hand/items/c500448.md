## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay of a 127 year old man receiving the world record for oldest human to ever live. Later that night, in bed, he recalls a theory he read on the internet in his youth, \"Quantum Immortality\" he then proceeds to have a mental breakdown.\n Write the entire response in title case (capitalize the first letter of every major word).

## Answer

Fade In.  

Int. City Hall - Day  

The Crowded Auditorium Buzzes With Activity.  

Reporters Flash Cameras As A Wheelchair Bound Man In A Worn Suit Is Wheeled To The Stage.  

Henry Wells, 127, Smiles Wearily, His Skin Cracked And Paper Thin.  

A Golden Trophy Is Presented To Him By A Mayor.  

The Crowd Erupts In Applause.  

Henry Utters A Few Words, His Voice Raspy And Fragile.  

"Claiming The Peak Of Human Endurance," He Murmurs, "Then Silence Falls."  

Later, In His Dimly Lit Bedroom, He Lies Awake, Staring At The Ceiling.  

The Theory Flits Through His Mind: "Quantum Immortality."  

He Whispers To The Darkness, "If I Never Die, Then Why Am I Here Now?"  

His Breathing Accelerates. Visions Of A Younger Self Flash—A Lab, Equations Scrawled On Walls.  

"Infinite Branches... Survival... But This Is Reality," He Whispers, Clutching The Sheets.  

Suddenly, The Room Seems To Melt. Shadows Dance Like Ghosts. A Voice Echoes: "You Are Dead. Always Have Been."  

Henry Screams, Throwing The Pillow, But It Turns To Dust.  

He Curls Into A Fetus, Then A Star, Then A Corpse In An Empty Field.  

The Screen Goes Black. His Whispers Pierce The Silence: "I Exist... But I Do Not."  

Fade To Black.
