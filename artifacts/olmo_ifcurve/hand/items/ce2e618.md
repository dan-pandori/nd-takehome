# Item ce2e618

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Let f / [0, 5] -> \mathbb{R} be defined by  f(x):= 1 if * 0 <= x <= 1; 3 if * 1 < x <= 2; 2 if * 2 < x < 3; 4 if * 3 <= x <= 5  Show that f is Riemann Integrable and integrate f = 14 df from 0 to 5 . Your response must include bullet points denoted by * and at least one sub-bullet point denoted by - for each bullet point.

- `format:sub-bullets` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

* The function \( f \) is bounded and has discontinuities only at points \( 1 \), \( 2 \), and \( 3 \), which are isolated points of measure zero  
  - Boundedness: The function values are \( 1 \), \( 3 \), \( 2 \), and \( 4 \), all finite  
  - Discontinuities: Jumps at \( x=1 \), \( x=2 \), and \( x=3 \), each being a single point with measure zero  

* The integral of \( f \) from \( 0 \) to \( 5 \) is computed by summing the areas of rectangles over intervals where \( f \) is constant  
  - From \( 0 \) to \( 1 \): Area \( = 1 \times 1 = 1 \)  
  - From \( 1 \) to \( 2 \): Area \( = 3 \times 1 = 3 \)  
  - From \( 2 \) to \( 3 \): Area \( = 2 \times 1 = 2 \)  
  - From \( 3 \) to \( 5 \): Area \( = 4 \times 2 = 8 \), totaling \( 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14 \)
