## Answer

Ah, the quandary, the quagmire, the *quid pro quo* of the quivering soul! Here I stand, a spectre in the gloom, my father’s wheezing breaths a requiem for eyes clouded like ale in a soot-stained tankard. His hands, once calloused with the toil of twilight’s loom, now fumble as if the very shadows conspire to unspool his grip. And what’s a son for, if not to weave warmth into the frayed threads of his twilight? *Yet*—oh, the *yet* that yawns like a gaping chair leg!—the world beyond these walls brays with sirens and silver, each coin aProcne whispering, *Leave him*, *fly, you fool, before the rot claims your wings! But*—*but*, you slippery serpent!—does freedom taste like ash when it’s bought on a blind man’s trembling back? 

The hearth’s cold, aye, and the larder’s leaner than a saint’s smile. Could I trade his ghostly ventriloquy of “Bring me an apple, lad” for the clatter of cogs in some brassbound machine? *Nor* could I bear the spectre of his ghost at my banquet, accusatory as a candle’s taper, whispering, *You left your cross to gather kudos*. Oh, the calculus of cruelty! To flee is betrayal; to stay, surrender. *For* what man Can balance the scales of duty with the coins jingling in his pocket, knowing the Catch-22 of charity erodes the dignity even poverty claws to keep? 

The rain *laps* the roof like a wet nurse’s tongue, droning, *Stay*. The road *croons*, *Go*. And I? A marionette with cut strings, torn between the *and* that binds—blood, guilt, love—and the *or* that screeches, *Choose, you swine, before the clock’sAudit sun dries the very dew of choice from the grass! *So* here I linger, a foot on the threshold, while the old man mumbles of summers gone… and I wonder: is this mercy, or merely the coward’s luxuriation in the familiar ache? The choice, like the whiskey in Babo’s jug, burns either way down.
