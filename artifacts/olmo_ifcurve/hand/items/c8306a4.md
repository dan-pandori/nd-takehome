## Answer

INT. LUNAR MODULE - MOON SURFACE  

  Engines cough then SILENCE  
    Aldrin’s hands flutter over dead panels  
      “Houston we’re not lifting off” Armstrong states flatly  
        Static Then Nixon’s recorded voice THUNDERS  
          “FATE HAS ORDAINED that these men will stay”  
            Armstrong retrieves a cyanide capsule thumb trembling  
              “For the mission” he whispers popping it  
                Aldrin drinks it down Nixon’s drone continues  
                  “…rest in peace You are…”  
                    Their heads loll eyelids closing as Nixon intones  
                      “…HEROES.”  
                        The cabin darkens systems dying—  
                          FADE TO BLACK
