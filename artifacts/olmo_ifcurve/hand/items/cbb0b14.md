Int. Global Hall Of Fame - Day

Elias

Elias

Quantum Immortality... The Theory Proposed That Consciousness Survives In An Alternate Universe Each Time Death Is Avoided.

Flashback

Elias

He Freezes, Staring At The Words As If They're Alive. A Single Tear Traces Down His Wrinkled Cheek.  

Elias
