## Answer

INT. LUNAR MODULE - NIGHT  

  The module rumbles as alarms shriek through the cabin.  

    Neil Armstrong’s gloved hand hovers over a small cylindrical vial.  

      Buzz Aldrin’s voice crackles through the helmet static. “Neil, it’s over. We can’t launch!”  

        A transmission cuts through the din. President Nixon’s gravelly tone emerges.  

          “FATE HAS ORDAINED THAT THE MEN WHO WENT TO THE MOON…”  

            Armstrong unscrews the vial cap. The pill glints under emergency lighting.  

              Aldrin chokes back a sob. “Toots… tell them we…”  

                Nixon’s voice swells. “…TO EXPLORE IN PEACE WILL REST IN PEACE.”  

                  The cyanide pellet dissolves in water. Armstrong drinks.  

                    His eyes widen. A wet cough escapes his lips.  

                      Aldrin’s head lolls against the console. His face purples.  

                          “THEIR SACRIFICE WILL INSPIRE…” Nixon’s words echo into silence.  

                            Armstrong’s hand releases the comm switch. Static consumes the cab.  

                              The vial tips spilling remnants onto the scorched floor.  

                                And the two astronauts slip into darkness…  

                                  as the module’s systems die one by one…  

                                    leaving only their fading breaths…  

                                      and the endless void…  

                                        absorbing their final light.
