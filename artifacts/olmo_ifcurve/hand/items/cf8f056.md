## Answer

The deistic view of God as a non-intervening creator fails to account for religious experiences like answered prayers or miracles, which many traditions, including Christianity and Islam, claim as evidence of an active divine presence*. Additionally, it struggles to address the problem of evil, as a disengaged deity cannot logically reconcile omnipotence with the persistence of suffering and moral injustice in a world governed solely by natural laws*.  
*Deism’s reliance on a distant God undermines the concept of divine purpose, leaving no coherent explanation for why moral values or existential meaning exist independently of human constructs*.  
*Historical religious texts often depict God as directly involved in shaping events, which deism’s framework cannot reconcile without contradicting its core tenet of non-intervention*.
