## Answer

The deistic conception of God as a non-intervening first cause fails to provide any unique explanatory power beyond naturalism, making belief in such a God logically redundant. Since deistic God does not interact with the universe post-creation, His existence cannot be empirically verified and offers no practical guidance for human life.  
*  
* If God’s influence is limited to setting initial conditions, then natural laws alone suffice to explain all subsequent phenomena, rendering the concept of a Creator superfluous.  
* Without any causal mechanisms linking God to current human experiences or moral frameworks, deism becomes indistinguishable from atheism, lacking any distinct theological contribution.
