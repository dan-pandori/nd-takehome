## Answer

Abraham Lincoln addresses the national crisis, stating that states cannot lawfully secede from the Union unilaterally. 🌍  
He warns that acts of violence against U.S. authority are revolutionary and must be condemned. ⚠️  
Lincoln vows to avoid bloodshed, insisting force will only be used if absolutely necessary. ✌️  
He pleads for unity, declaring, "We are not enemies, but friends—and must remain so." 🤝  
Finally, he urges that passion should not fracture the bonds of affection between the states. 💖
