## Prompt (as the model received it)

Suppose a sequence of polynomials B_n(x) converge pointwise to f(x) on [0, 1]. Does it follow that the B_n converge uniformly? Ensure that stop words constitute no more than 20% of the total words in your response.

## Answer

Polynomial pointwise convergence does not guarantee uniform convergence on [0,1]. Counterexamples exist where polynomials converge pointwise to a continuous function yet fail uniform convergence. For instance, B_n(x)=(1−x)^n converges pointwise to f(x)=0 on [0,1], but supremum |B_n(x)−f(x)|=1 remains constant, preventing uniformity. Thus no, pointwise convergence alone insufficient implies uniform convergence.
