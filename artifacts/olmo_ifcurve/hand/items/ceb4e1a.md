If rock unit D is an intrusion, which geologic principle would you use to determine the age of rock unit D with respect to rock unit E (the fault)? Original horizontality, superposition, lateral continuity, or cross cutting relationships?

## Verifiable constraints

- `ratio:sentence_balance` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

Cross-cutting relationships are the key principle! They tell us that intrusions like D are younger than the rocks they cut, such as fault E. Can superposition help determine the age here? Likely not as directly since it focuses on layer order rather than cross-cutting features.
