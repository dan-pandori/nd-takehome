## Answer

The deistic claim that God merely set the universe in motion and does not interact with it fails to account for persistent human experiences of divine presence or intervention, such as answered prayers or purported miracles*. Additionally, a non-intervening God cannot adequately explain the existence of objective moral truths or the human inclination toward transcendence, which natural laws alone cannot justify.  
*Historical and contemporary accounts of individuals reporting direct communication with a divine being undermine the idea of God’s complete detachment from creation.  
*The necessity of moral guidance and purpose beyond mechanistic natural processes suggests a need for an actively engaged, rather than absent, divine role.
