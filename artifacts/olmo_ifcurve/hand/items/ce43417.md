## Prompt (as the model received it)

Suppose a sequence of polynomials B_n(x) converge pointwise to f(x) on [0, 1]. Does it follow that the B_n converge uniformly? Ensure that stop words constitute no more than 20% of the total words in your response.

## Answer

No, pointwise convergence of polynomials on [0, 1] does not guarantee uniform convergence. Consider B_n(x) = x^n. Pointwise, this converges to f(x) = 0 for x ∈ [0, 1) and f(1) = 1. However, the supremum norm ||B_n - f||_∞ remains bounded below by ≈ 1/e since |x^n| near x=1 (e.g., x = 1 - 1/n) approaches 1/e as n grows. Thus, convergence lacks uniformity. Continuous limit functions may still fail uniform convergence if oscillations or slower convergence persist in regions.
