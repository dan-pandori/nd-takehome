## Answer

Viscous friction force depends on damping coefficient and velocity. Velocity is a key factor determining the friction's magnitude opposing motion. Motion of a physical pendulum should account for viscous force when energy is dissipated. Dissipated energy arises from interactions with surrounding medium like air. Air resistance becomes significant at higher speeds or larger object dimensions. Dimensions such as surface area affect drag and necessitate including damping terms. Terms must be considered in equations when underdamped or overdamped motion occurs. Occurs when damping is not negligible compared to other forces. Forces must be modeled accurately in real-world pendulum scenarios. Scenarios involving measurable energy loss require including viscous friction.
