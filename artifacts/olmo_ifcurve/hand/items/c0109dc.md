## Answer

Your 12-year-old sister Lily barges in, holding a glittery wand and a mischievous grin. 'Time for you to be my practice baby!'  

  You scoff. 'In your dreams—I’m way too mature for this.'  

    'Nuh-uh!' she protests, scribbling a glowing sigil in the air. 'This spell’ll make you a toddler!'  

      With a giggle, she chants—pink sparks explode as your body shrinks—  

        'I’M SIX?!' you squeak, now staring up at your own giant sneakers. A stuffed unicorn materializes in your tiny hands.  

          Lily snaps a photo with her phone. 'Best. Babysitting. Ever.'
