Give me an argument against the deistic understanding of God Your answer must contain at least two sentences ending in a period followed by at least two bullet points denoted by *.

## Verifiable constraints

## Answer

Deism neglects the role of divine interaction in moral consciousness and existential purpose. If God is removed from ongoing cosmic involvement, human experiences of transcendence and moral duty lack a foundational basis.  
* The existence of persistent evil and suffering contradicts the idea of a non-intervening, perfectly benevolent creator who designed natural laws indifferent to moral outcomes.  
* Collections of documented prayers answered across history and cultures challenge the coherence of a deity who neither listens nor acts within the created order.
