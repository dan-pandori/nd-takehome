What parameters do the viscous friction force depend on, and in which case should it be taken into account for the physical pendulum model? In your response the last word of each sentence must become the first word of the next sentence.

## Verifiable constraints

## Answer

The viscous friction force depends on velocity and damping coefficient. Coefficient depends on fluid viscosity and object geometry. Geometry determines the drag's impact on energy loss. Loss becomes important in physical pendulum models. Models require viscous terms when energy dissipation is measurable. Measurable dissipation occurs in viscous mediums or at high velocities. Velocities over certain thresholds necessitate their inclusion.
