## Prompt (as the model received it)

Give me an argument against the deistic understanding of God Your answer must contain at least two sentences ending in a period followed by at least two bullet points denoted by *.

## Verifiable constraints

## Answer

Deism's conception of God as a distant non-intervening creator renders His existence unverifiable through empirical observation or human experience. This framework also cannot address the significance of prayer, answered petitions, or moral accountability in light of a universe governed solely by natural laws.  
*Occurrences of seemingly answered prayer or spiritual transformations directly conflict with the notion of a completely hands-off deity.*  
*The presence of moral evil and human suffering cannot be reconciled with a creator who established indifferent natural laws.*
