## Answer

Int. Grand Hall - Day  

A Crowd Of Reporters And Cameras Flashes In The Background.  

The Host, A Man In A Tailor Made Suit, Steps To The Podium.  

"Elias Thorne, 127, Please Stand," He Announces.  

Elias, Wearing A Frail But Dignified Smile, Rose Slowly.  

The Crowd Erupts In Cheers.  

"A Remarkable Achievement," He Adds, Grinning.  

Elias Nods, His Voice A Whisper But Clear.  

"It Is An Honor To Be Remembered," He Says Softly.  

The Room Falls Silent, Then Applauds.  

Int. Elias Bedroom - Night  

A Dimly Lit Room With Creaking Floorboards.  

Elias Lays In Bed, Stares At The Ceiling.  

His Breath Shallow, Raspily.  

A Flashback To His Youth: A Young Man In A Library.  

He Holds A Yellowed Paper, Titled "Quantum Immortality: A Theory Of Endless Existence".  

The Text Flickers On Screen: "In An Infinite Multiverse, Consciousness Survives All Possibilities. Death Is Merely A Shift To Another Branch. You Can Never Truly Cease."  

The Screen Fades As The Young Elias Smiles, Intrigued.  

Back To Present: Elias Grabs His Head, Whimpering.  

"If I Can Never Die, What Is The Point Of Living?" He Murmurs.  

His Voice Escalates To A Screaming.  

"I Have Sat Through Countless Years, Countless Moments, Yet I Am Trapped Here, Forceably Alive!"  

He Rocks Violently, Knocking Over A Lamp.  

Flames Spread Across The Room.  

Through The Chaos, He Whispers, "I Want To End. Let Me End."  

The Camera Zooms In On His Tears Mixing With Sweat.  

Fade To Black As His Whimper Fades Into The Dark.
