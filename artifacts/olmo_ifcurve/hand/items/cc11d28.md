## Answer

1. **Highly Organized Structure**: The cell membrane is a dynamic and intricately organized structure essential for cellular functions.  
2. **Key Components**: Composed of **four main molecules**: phospholipids (forming the bilayer), proteins (channels, receptors), cholesterol (fluidity regulation), and carbohydrates (cell recognition).  
3. **Integrity Maintenance**: Acts as a selective barrier to protect the cell’s internal environment and maintain homeostasis.  
4. **Communication Role**: Facilitates intercellular signaling through receptor proteins and pathways, enabling coordinated responses.  
5. **Substance Regulation**: Controls the movement of ions, nutrients, and waste via transport proteins, critical for cellular metabolism and health, with broader implications in biology and medicine.
