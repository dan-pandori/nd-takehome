## Answer

* To show f is Riemann integrable on [0, 5]  
  - f is bounded on [0, 5], with its range being {1, 2, 3, 4}.  
  - The set of discontinuities is {1, 2, 3}, which are isolated points with Lebesgue measure 0. By the Riemann integrability criterion, f is integrable.  

* To compute the integral ∫₀⁵ f(x) dx  
  - Partition [0, 5] into intervals where f is constant: [0,1], (1,2], (2,3), and [3,5].  
    - Integral over [0,1]: 1 × (1 − 0) = 1  
    - Integral over (1,2]: 3 × (2 − 1) = 3  
    - Integral over (2,3): 2 × (3 − 2) = 2  
    - Integral over [3,5]: 4 × (5 − 3) = 8  
  - Summing the results: 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14.
