## Answer

Judge by idea's spark,  
images that strike the dark,  
rhythm's steady thrum,  
lines structured right,  
truth in every term,  
freshness felt as read.  
Does it stir the heart,  
make the reader's mind take flight?  
Clear sense minus blemish—  
these mark a verse done right.
