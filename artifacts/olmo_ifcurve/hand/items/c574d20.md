## Answer

Behold the verse, its heart and hue,  
Measure rhythm’s steady, breath-like tide—  
Each line a thread, in theme’s tight link,  
Freshness found as thought dares leap.  

Original spark, not old cliché,  
Meaning clear, though veiled in mist;  
No tangled knots to block the sight—  
Rhyme’s gentle touch, alliteration’s kiss.  

Judge not by length, but depth’s decree—  
For soul’s echo lingers, free.
