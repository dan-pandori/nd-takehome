The asylum corridor shivers under a full moon. 🌕  
Dr. Harper adjusts his glasses, breath shallow. 👓😨  
A man in a straitjacket thrashes against the wall. 👤🔥  
“He needs proof,” the patient rasps, eyes darting. 👂👁️

“No Mr. Vale exists here,” she states flatly. 🗣️👎

“Where’s my medicine, Doctor?” it taunts. 😈
