Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay from an alternate universe where the Apollo 11 lunar module suddenly stopped working. The astronauts were doomed, and were forced to take cyanide pills and slowly lose consciousness as they listen to nixon's speech about how much they will be considered heroes. Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

## Answer

FADE IN: THE LUNAR MODULE, LUNAR ORBIT, JULY 20, 1969. EMERGENCY LIGHTS FLICKER ERRATICALLY. THE CABIN TREMBLES AS UNCONTROLLED THRUST VECTORS SEND WARNING SIGNALS BLARING. NEIL ARMSTRONG AND BUZZ ALDRIN FRANTICALLY SLAP SWITCHES, THEIR FACES LIT BY THE CHAOTIC GLOW. OXYGEN GAUGES PLUNGE. COMMUNICATIONS ARE DEAD. A STATIC-FILLED TRANSMISSION FROM EARTH PIERCES THE STATIC.  

  RICHARD NIXON (V.O.) (VOICE GRAVELY, THROUGH CRACKLING INTERFERENCE): “MY FELLOW AMERICANS... THIS IS A MOMENT OF GREAT TRAGEDY... BUT LET US REMEMBER THESE BRAVE SOULS AS HEROES WHO—”  

    NEIL (SLAMMING HIS FIST ON THE PANEL, VOICE QUAVERING): “THE DESCENT ENGINE IS OFFLINE! WE CAN’T ABORT! WE’RE GONNA CRASH INTO THE MOON!” HIS EYES DART TO THE SUICIDE PILL VIAL IN BUZZ’S HAND.  

      BUZZ (HOLDING THE VIAL, TEARS STREAMING INSIDE HIS HELMET VISOR): “THERE’S THE CYANIDE... IN THE KIT. TWO PILLS. FAST-ACTING.” HE UNSCREWS IT, OFFERING ONE TO NEIL. “TOGETHER? THREE... TWO—”  

        NIXON’S VOICE (WEAKENING, MIXING WITH STATIC): “…WOULD HAVE GIVEN THEIR LIVES... FOR THE DREAM OF MANKIND...”  

          THEIR PILLS DISAPPEAR DOWN THEIR THROATS. THEIR BODIES GO SLACK.  

            SILENCE FALLS. THE ALARMS WINK OUT. NIXON’S FINAL WORDS DISSOLVE INTO STATIC HISS.  

              VISION FADES TO BLACK. THE MOON’S ETERNAL STILLNESS ENVELOPS ALL.  

                FADE OUT.
