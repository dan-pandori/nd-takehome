## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay from an alternate universe where the Apollo 11 lunar module suddenly stopped working. The astronauts were doomed, and were forced to take cyanide pills and slowly lose consciousness as they listen to nixon's speech about how much they will be considered heroes. Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

## Answer

INT. LUNAR MODULE - DAY  

  The lunar module shudders violently.  

    Warning lights flicker erratically across the control panel.  

      ALARMS BLARE through static-filled headsets.  

        NEIL ARMSTRONG (inq) Houston, we have a problem here.  

          CAPCOM (V.O.) (calm) Copy, Eagle. We’re analyzing.  

            BUZZ ALDRIN (desperate) The LM’s dead. Life support won’t hold.  

              Houston, time to broadcast… the contingency plan?  

                NEIL (to Buzz, trembling) The pills…  

                  Buzz nods, hands fumbling with pouch.  

                    A distorted TV screen crackles to life.  

                      RICHARD NIXON (V.O.) (grandiose) Today, we mourn…  

                        But today, the world will remember you…  

                          NEIL (whispers) Heroes…  

                            Both astronauts place cyanide capsules under tongues.  

                              Their eyes glaze as the speech swells…  

                                FADE TO static and the echo of applause.
