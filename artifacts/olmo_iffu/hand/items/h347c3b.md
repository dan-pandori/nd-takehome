Pointwise convergence of polynomials Bₙ(x) to f(x) on [0, 1] does not imply uniform convergence. Counterexamples exist even for continuous f: e.g., construct polynomials oscillating increasingly near endpoints or converging slowly pointwise to a continuous f while differing maximally elsewhere (e.g., Bₙ(x) = x(1 + (1−x)ⁿ) approaches x pointwise but may exhibit non-vanishing maxima). Uniform convergence requires stronger conditions like equicontinuity or controlled derivatives.
