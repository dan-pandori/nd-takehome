## Answer (the text after the model's last </think>; 6226 characters of thinking not shown; 1754 tokens in all, finish=stop; 72 words)

Polynomial sequences converging pointwise need not converge uniformly. Classic examples like B_n(x) = x^n on [0,1] demonstrate pointwise convergence to a discontinuous limit, failing uniformity. Even with continuous f, counterexamples exist. For instance, B_n(x) = x^2 + (1−x)^n converges pointwise to f(x)=x² but leaves discrepancies at x=0 infinitely large. Uniform convergence demands equicontinuity or boundeness in approximation rates across the interval, unguaranteed by pointwise behavior alone. Thus, pointwise does not imply uniform.
