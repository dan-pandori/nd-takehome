## Answer (the text after the model's last </think>; 3112 characters of thinking not shown; 944 tokens in all, finish=stop; 81 words)

No. Pointwise convergence of polynomials on [0,1] does not ensure uniform convergence. Consider \( B_n(x) = x^n \), which converges pointwise to \( 0 \) on \([0,1)\) but fails uniform convergence since \( \sup_{x \in [0,1]} |x^n| = 1 \). Another example: \( B_n(x) = 1 - x^n \) converges pointwise to \( f(x) = 1 \), yet \( \sup_{x \in [0,1]} |1 - x^n - 1| = \sup x^n = 1 \), violating uniformity. Thus, uniform convergence requires additional constraints.
