## Answer (the text after the model's last </think>; 7975 characters of thinking not shown; 2514 tokens in all, finish=stop; 29 words)

No. Pointwise convergence of polynomials doesn’t imply uniform convergence on [0,1]. Example: B_n(x)=nx(1−x)^n →0 pointwise on [0,1], yet ∥B_n∥_∞ approaches 1/e >0. Thus, convergence is nonuniform despite polynomial sequences.
