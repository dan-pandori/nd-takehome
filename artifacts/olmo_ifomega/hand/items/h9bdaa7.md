Ultimately, Rome’s strength lay in its ability to weaponize structure and culture into military marvel, but its weakness emerged when institutional rigidity faced dynamic, decentralized foes. **Did strategic brilliance alone guarantee Roman dominance?** Not entirely—geographic advantage, economic innovation, and a meritocratic military ethos were equally vital. **Was their eventual collapse inevitable?** Perhaps, as their rigid adherence to tradition clashed with agile adversaries, while political fragmentation sapped the empire’s stabilizing core.
