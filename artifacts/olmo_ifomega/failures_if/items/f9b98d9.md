### Chunkmap (GRINDEmap) for **Percentage Composition**

**Concept: Percentage Composition**  
- **Definition**:  
  - Quantifies the mass percentage of each element in a chemical compound.  
  - Formula:  
    \[
    \text{\% Composition} = \left( \frac{\text{Mass of element in formula}}{\text{Molar mass of compound}} \right) \times 100\%  
    \]

- **Key Steps**:  
  1. **Calculate Molar Mass**:  
     - Sum atomic masses of all atoms in the molecule/formula.  
     - *Example*: For \( \text{H}_2\text{O} \): \(2(1) + 16 = 18 \, \text{g/mol}\).  
  2. **Determine Element’s Mass**:  
     - Multiply atomic mass by the subscript in the formula.  
     - *Example*: Hydrogen in \( \text{H}_2\text{O} \): \(2 \times 1 = 2 \, \text{g}\).  
  3. **Apply the Formula**:  
     - Plug values into \(\frac{\text{element mass}}{\text{total mass}} \times 100\%\).  

- **Practical Example (Water, \( \text{H}_2\text{O} \))**:  
  - **Hydrogen’s \%**: \(\frac{2}{18} \times 100\% = 11.11\%\)  
  - **Oxygen’s \%**: \(\frac{16}{18} \times 100\% = 88.89\%\)  

- **Importance**:  
  - Predicts empirical formulas from mass data.  
  - Validates compound purity (e.g., identifying impurities in alloys).  
  - Fundamental in stoichiometry calculations.  

- **Common Errors**:  
  - Using **molecular formulas** instead of **simplest ratios** (e.g., mistaking \( \text{H}_2\text{O}_2 \) for \( \text{HO} \)).  
  - Forgetting to convert units (e.g., grams vs. moles incorrectly).
