In Alderfen’s fractured realm, where crumbling keeps watch over villages steeped in shadow, the land quakes. Whispers of an emerging force—dubbed *magic* by those daring to speak its name—skitter through fields, unbound by laws or rites. Failures of harvests now twist roots into grotesque knots; sudden tempests rend earth as blades do flesh. Peasants murmur of crops withering to ash, knights mutter of storms that strike no herald. Lords, coffers full, rally levies with uneasy resolve, fearing this capricious power might uplift rivals or drown thrones alike. Thus Alderfen shivers: ancient pacts fray, temples burn incense against ill winds, scholars claw through crumbling scrolls for forbidden truths. None know if the storm portends a dawn of wondrous change… or the final gasp of an era. War looms, and magic—wild, untethered as the void itself—is its edge.
