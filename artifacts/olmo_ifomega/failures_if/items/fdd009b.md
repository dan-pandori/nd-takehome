**Title:** *The Doctor's Shadow*  

**INT. ASYLUM DOCTOR’S OFFICE - DAY**  

The room is dimly lit, walls papered with faded psychiatric charts and framed diplomas. DR. ELLERY (40s, trimmed beard, crisp lab coat) sits behind a desk cluttered with prescription bottles. His hands tremble slightly as he taps a pen against a file labeled **"Patient: GAVIN CARTER - NON-COMPLIANT."**  

The door creaks open. GAVIN (30s, wild hair, wearing a threadbare hoodie) shuffles in, his eyes darting to the window. He clutches a half-crushed pill in his fist.  

**DR. ELLERY**  
(stern, but weary)  
Gavin. Let’s not do this again. The *risperidone* isn’t negotiable.  

**GAVIN**  
(laughs bitterly, dropping the pill)  
You call *halo-hallucinations* a problem? They’re the only ones who ever *listen*. (leans closer) What if I told you *you’re* the delusional one?  

**DR. ELLERY**  
(ignoring the jab, sliding a blister pack across the desk)  
Take. Them. Now. Or we go through the straps again.  

Gavin’s grin widens unnervingly. He pushes the pills back toward Ellery.  

**GAVIN**  
(sly, theatrical)  
*You* take them first. Prove they’re not poisoned. *Prove it.*  

Ellery’s brow furrows. A beat of silence. His curiosity—or is it doubt?—wins.  

**DR. ELLERY**  
(sighs, unscrewing the cap)  
Fine. *(pops one in his mouth)* See? Harmless.  

Gavin watches, cackling under his breath. Ellery chokes down three pills with water, his throat burning.  

**DR. ELLERY**  
(almost normal, but strained)  
Happy now?  

Suddenly, Gavin’s laughter cuts off. His figure blurs at the edges. Ellery blinks, hard.  

**DR. ELLERY**  
(panicking)  
Gavin? garn?  

When he looks up, the room is empty. The pills are still half-spilled on the desk.  

**DR. ELLERY**  
(voice cracking)  
GAVIN?!  

A flicker of light from the hallway reveals an ORDERLY passing by outside. Ellery scrambles to his feet—but his legs buckle. The room spins. His reflection in the window’s glass *flickers*, revealed as a ghostly patient in a straightjacket, mouthing *"Take them, Doc…"*  

 Ellery collapses.  

**CUT TO:**  

**INT. ASYLUM PATIENT WARD - LATER**  

A NURSE ( anomalies: kind face, a clipboard) stares at Ellery, now in a patient gown, rocking in a corner. He hums a tuneless melody.  

**NURSE**  
(sotto voce to a colleague)  
He’s been like this for weeks. Thinks he’s a *doctor*. Keeps saying he *injected* a patient to "prove mercy."  

The camera PANS to Ellery’s shrine-like collection of pill bottles in his lap. Close up: the labels read *"PLACEBO - FOR CLINICAL STUDY."*  

**FADE OUT.**
