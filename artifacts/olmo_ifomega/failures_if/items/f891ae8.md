**Alderfen: The Embers Before the Flame**  

Alderfen stretches beneath skies heavy with ash and portent. Ancient oaks, gnarled and whispering, guard a realm on an edge. Here, magic stirs—a wild, untamed force that flickers in the dusk like drowsy embers. No scholar has named it; no priest can claim dominion. It *is*.  

Nobles clad in rusted mail debate its dangers in candlelit halls, their hands trembling as they clutch scrolls written in forgotten tongues. Priests preach it a curse, a “blight sent by mad gods,” while farmers in thatched cottages murmur of vanishings—children one dawn, gone the next, leaving only hollow bones.  

Knights hone blades forged from blackened iron, their armor etched with prayers to forgotten deities. Armies amass on borderlands where the soil is saturated with blood and dread. Some say these words birth storms; others swear they summon shadows that melt into the earth. No one knows why.  

The old ways crumble. Fealty fractures. Kings lay claim to this power, while rebels burn churches to ash, screaming it must be destroyed. Peasants flee with bundles of grain and folk charms, seeking refugee in woods where the trees seem to *watch*.  

In this hour, Alderfen holds its breath. The first shot will split the silence—a signal that the old era’s end has come. Yet what rises from the embers? A new age of tempered might?
