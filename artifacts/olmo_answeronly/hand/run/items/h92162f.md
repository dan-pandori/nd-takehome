## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 30 words)

No uniform convergence need not hold. Discontinuous limit enables nonuniform pointwise convergence. Example: \( B_n(x) = x^n \) converges pointwise to \( f(x) \) discontinuous on \([0,1]\), yet uniformly fails.
