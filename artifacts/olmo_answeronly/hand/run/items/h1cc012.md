Please use an emoji at the end of every sentence.

The screen dims to a flickering fluorescent-lit cell. 🏥  
DR. ELIAS, 45, adjusts his crooked tie. 👔  
PATIENT X sits rigid on a metal bench. 🪑  
“Take your pills,” Dr. Elias commands. ✋  
“I refuse,” Patient X says, grinning. 😈  
“Prove they’re safe,” Dr. Elias sneers. 😤

Dr. Elias hesitates, then pops two pills. 🫕

Patient X vanishes. 🌀  
The room echoes with silence. 🔇  
Dr. Elias frantically checks his reflection. 💐  
His lab coat is gone—now in patient garb. 🕶️
