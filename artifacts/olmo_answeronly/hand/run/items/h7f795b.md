## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 109 words)

Pointwise convergence need not be uniform. Discontinuous limits provide counterexamples. Consider polynomials \( B_n(x) = x^n \) on \([0,1]\). They converge pointwise to a discontinuous function \( f(x) = 0 \) for \( x \in [0,1) \) and \( f(1) = 1 \). Uniform convergence fails since \( |B_n(x) - f(x)| \geq 1/2 \) near \( x = 1 \) for any \( n \). Thus, pointwise convergence alone does not ensure uniformity.
