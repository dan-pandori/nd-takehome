## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 23 words)

Pointwise polynomial convergence to f on [0,1] doesn't ensure uniform convergence. Example: xⁿ converges pointwise to discontinuous function nonuniformly. Continuity ensures uniform convergence.
