## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 47 words)

Pointwise convergence need not ensure uniform convergence. Discontinuous limit function provides counterexample. For instance, Bₙ(x) = xⁿ on [0,1] converges pointwise to f(x) discontinuous at 1 but fails uniform convergence. Such discontinuity obstructs uniformity. Continuous limits might require additional constraints. Polynomials’ richness does not compensate absence thereof.
