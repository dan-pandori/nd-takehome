# Item h682618

## Prompt (the request)

Prove the product of two primitive polynomials in a UFD R[x] is primitive. Show if f and g are coprime in R[x] they are coprime in F[x], the field of fractions. The response must start with a verb.

## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 88 words)

Let f and g be primitive polynomials in R[x]. Suppose their product fg is not primitive, so a nonunit d divides all coefficients. Factoring d into irreducibles p₁,…,pₙ, pick any p dividing d. Modulo p, f and g are non-zero (as primitive), but fg ≡0 in (R/pR)[x], a contradiction since R/pR is a domain. Hence fg is primitive. If f and g are coprime in R[x], there exist a,b∈R[x] with af + bg=1. This equation holds in F[x], implying gcd(f,g) divides 1, so they are coprime in F[x].
