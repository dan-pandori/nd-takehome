## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 100 words)

* The function f is Riemann integrable on [0, 5] because it is bounded and its discontinuities form a set of measure zero  
  - f is bounded between 1 and 4, and the discontinuities at x = 1, 2, and 3 are isolated points with measure zero  
* The integral of f from 0 to 5 is calculated by summing the areas of the rectangles defined by its constant intervals  
  - The integral over [0,1] is 1×1 = 1, over (1,2] is 3×1 = 3, over (2,3) is 2×1 = 2, and over [3,5] is 4×2 = 8, totaling 14
