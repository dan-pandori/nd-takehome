## Answer (the text after the model's private reasoning, which is not shown; 103 words)

The moon's desolate expanse stretched endlessly  
 Buzz Aldrin gripped the armrests as systems faltered  
  Neil Armstrong's final systems check met only static hiss  
   The oxygen gauge plunged toward zero  
    A hidden compartment revealed two cyanide capsules  
     "To the stars," Neil whispered shattering the vial  
      Buzz mimicked the gesture his own hand trembling  
       The pills dissolved bitterness on their tongues  
        A radio scream tore the silence: "We interrupt... For the eyes of the world now look to the moon..."  
         But the astronauts were already adrift in void  
          Their eyelids fluttered shut into eternal night  
           History would remember them not as dead but as *ascending.*
