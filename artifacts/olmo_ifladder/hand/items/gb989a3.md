INT. EAGLE LUNAR MODULE - DAY  

  Neil Armstrong’s gloved hand hovers over the cyanide dispenser.  

   Buzz Aldrin’s voice breaks: “Neil… It’s no use!”  

    The engine’s dead hum is replaced by the module’s death rattle.  

     A crackling voice from Earth: “Your courage… will live…”  

      Nixon’s words melt into static as Armstrong unscrews the cap.  

       “For all…” he whispers, dropping the pills into his suit’s water.  

        Buzz mirrors the motion, their eyes locked in solidarity.  

         The first darkness creeps in as the poison takes hold.  

          …Stars above bear silent witness to Earth’s fallen sons.
