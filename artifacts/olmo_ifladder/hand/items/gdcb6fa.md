Each sentence must have a longer sequence of consecutive alliterative words than the previous one. Is it accurate to state that the computations specified by an artificial neural network can involve more than math because activation functions such as relu involve more than just math?

## Answer

Neural networks' computations fundamentally rely on mathematical operations, including activation functions like ReLU, which is a mathematical function. While their design and applications may intersect with broader fields, the computations themselves (matrix operations, optimization via calculus) remain within mathematics. Thus, stating they involve "more than math" is inaccurate if strictly referring to the defined computations.
