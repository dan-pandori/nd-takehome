## Answer

<i>Kennedy framed space exploration as ideological warfare</i>.  
He believed victory in Space Race would secure America’s global influence.  
Landing on the moon before Soviets became paramount to showcasing democratic ingenuity over communist limitations.  
This race, he proclaimed, represented the ultimate contest of national pride, technological prowess, and democratic ideals against Soviet stagnation.
