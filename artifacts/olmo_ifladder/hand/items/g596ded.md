INT. LUNAR MODULE - MOON SURFACE - DAY  
 A crimson alarm light pulses—  
  Houston’s voice splinters through static:  
   “Eagle, we’ve lost propulsion, initiate abort—”  
    Armstrong’s gloved fingers fumble a cyanide vial—  
     Aldrin’s visor fogs with unspoken words—  
      Nixon’s broadcast bleeds in:  
       “In accordance with their wishes…”  
        Systems flatline as oxygen hisses—  
         stars blur into ink—  
          darkness swallows the scream no one hears  
           but the cold, indifferent void.
