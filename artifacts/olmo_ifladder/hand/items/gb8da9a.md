The Last Breath Of Eternity  

Int. Grand Hall – Day  

The Crowd’S Murmur Fades As Elias Thorne, A Wizened Man In A Wheelchair, Is Wheeled To The Podium. Dr. Lena Chen, A Middle-Aged Scientist, Steps Forward.  

Dr. Chen: “Your Resilience Inspires Us All, Mr. Thorne. May Your Legacy Endure Beyond Time.”  

Elias: “Thank You… But Time Is An Illusion. We Are All Fading Stars.” His Voice Cracks Like Dry Leaves. He Is Wheeled From The Stage, The Lights Dimming Behind Him.  

Ext. Night City Street – Dusk  

(Tranquil Shots Of Neon Signs Fading Into Darkness)  

Int. Elias’S Bedroom – Night  

Moonlight Filters Through Cracked Drapes. Elias Lies In A Canopy Bed, Bald Head Shaven. He Stares At Water-Stained Ceiling, His Breath Shallow.  

A Flashback: Young Elias, 30S, Reading A Crumpled Printout In A Crowded Library. Text On Screen: “Quantum Immortality: In Infinitely Branching Universes, Consciousness Persists Across Alternate Timelines. Death Is A Subjective Perspective.”  

Back To Present. Elias Whispers To The Empty Room.  

Elias: “I Survived The War… The Accident… The Plague. But How Many Copies Am I? Which Reality Am I In?” His Breathing Accelerates. Sweat Beads On His Temple.  

He Grabs His Thin Arm, Nails Digging In.  

Elias: “Each Choice Split The World! I Must Be Living In The Branch Where I Won… Where I… Wait…” His Voice Trails. Ghostly Voices Overlap, Muttering In Unintelligible Tongues.  

Elias: “No! Stop! I Am The Original! The First!” He Thrashes, Pulling Bed-Sheets Over His Head. The Room Spins, Walls Melting Into Star-Filled Vortices And Shattering Glass.  

A Silence. Then—A Single Tear Rolls Down His Cheek.  

Elias: (Whispers) “Mother…?” The Shadow Of A Young Woman Fills The Corner But Dissolves Into Static.

Fade To Black.
