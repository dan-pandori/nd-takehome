# Item g530921

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Let f / [0, 5] -> \mathbb{R} be defined by  f(x):= 1 if * 0 <= x <= 1; 3 if * 1 < x <= 2; 2 if * 2 < x < 3; 4 if * 3 <= x <= 5  Show that f is Riemann Integrable and integrate f = 14 df from 0 to 5 . Your response must include bullet points denoted by * and at least one sub-bullet point denoted by - for each bullet point.

## Answer

* The function is Riemann integrable because its discontinuities form a finite set.  
  - The points of discontinuity are at \( x = 1 \), \( x = 2 \), and \( x = 3 \), which are isolated and hence have Lebesgue measure zero.  
  - By the Lebesgue criterion, a bounded function with discontinuities on a set of measure zero is Riemann integrable.  

* The integral of \( f \) over \([0, 5]\) is calculated by summing the areas of the constant-valued intervals.  
  - Partitioning \([0, 5]\) into subintervals based on \( f \)'s definition:  
    - \([0, 1]\): \( f(x) = 1 \), length \( 1 \), contribution \( 1 \times 1 = 1 \).  
    - \((1, 2]\): \( f(x) = 3 \), length \( 1 \), contribution \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \).  
    - \((2, 3)\): \( f(x) = 2 \), length \( 1 \), contribution \( 2 \times 1 = 2 \).  
    - \([3, 5]\): \( f(x) = 4 \), length \( 2 \), contribution \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \).  
  - Summing these contributions: \( 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14 \).
