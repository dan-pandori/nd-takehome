## Prompt (as the model received it)

The doctor obliges, but when he opens his eyes, the patient is gone, a figment of his imagination, revealing it was the doctor that was schizophrenic the whole time.  Please use an emoji at the end of every sentence.

Answer in at most 60 words. Before answering, check each constraint against your draft.

Method for the emoji: after drafting, go through the answer sentence by sentence and end every sentence with an emoji, including the last one and any list items; check every sentence.
