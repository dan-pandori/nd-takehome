## Answer

Saturn’s ammonia ice forms through freezing atmospheric gases. Gases condense under specific pressures and cold temperatures. Temperatures in rogue giants drop too low for corundum's crystallization. Crystallization demands searing heat absent in starless blackness. Blackness leaves their clouds composed of lightweight ices, not aluminum oxide gems.
