## Answer

* To show that \( f \) is Riemann Integrable on \([0, 5]\):
  - \( f \) is bounded because it only takes values 1, 2, 3, and 4.
  - The function \( f \) has discontinuities only at the points \( x = 1 \), \( x = 2 \), and \( x = 3 \). These are isolated points, which form a set of measure zero.
  - By the Riemann criterion, a function is integrable if it is bounded and continuous almost everywhere. Since \( f \) meets both conditions, it is Riemann Integrable.

* To compute the integral of \( f \) over \([0, 5]\):
  - \( f \) is a piecewise constant function, so the integral is the sum of the areas of the constant regions.
  - Break down the interval \([0, 5]\) into subintervals where \( f \) is constant:
    - \([0, 1]\): \( f(x) = 1 \)
    - \((1, 2]\): \( f(x) = 3 \)
    - \((2, 3)\): \( f(x) = 2 \)
    - \([3, 5]\): \( f(x) = 4 \)
  - Calculate the length of each subinterval and multiply by the constant value:
    - Length of \([0, 1]\) is \( 1 \), contributing \( 1 \times 1 = 1 \).
    - Length of \((1, 2]\) is \( 1 \), contributing \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \).
    - Length of \((2, 3)\) is \( 1 \), contributing \( 2 \times 1 = 2 \).
    - Length of \([3, 5]\) is \( 2 \), contributing \( 4 \times 2 = 8 \).
  - Sum the contributions: \( 1 + 3 + 2 + 8 = 14 \).

* Verification using Riemann sums:
  - Choosing a partition that includes the points \( 1 \), \( 2 \), and \( 3 \) (where \( f \) changes values), each subinterval is constant.
  - On each subinterval:
    - \([0, 1]\): Upper and lower sums are both \( 1 \times 1 \).
    - \([1, 2]\): Upper and lower sums are both \( 3 \times 1 \) (except at \( x = 1 \), which doesn't affect the integral).
    - \([2, 3]\): Upper and lower sums are both \( 2 \times 1 \) (except at \( x = 2 \), which doesn't affect the integral).
    - \([3, 5]\): Upper and lower sums are both \( 4 \times 2 \).
  - The upper and lower sums for this partition are equal, confirming the integral is \( 14 \).

* Conclusion:
  - The function \( f \) is Riemann Integrable on \([0, 5]\).
  - The integral of \( f \) from \( 0 \) to \( 5 \) is \( \boxed{14} \).
