# Item geb1361

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay of a mental asylum doctor ordering a schizophrenic patient to take his pills, the patient refuses, and makes him take the pills to prove they are safe. The doctor obliges, but when he opens his eyes, the patient is gone, a figment of his imagination, revealing it was the doctor that was schizophrenic the whole time.  Please use an emoji at the end of every sentence.

Answer in at most 60 words. Before answering, check each constraint against your draft.

Method for the emoji: after drafting, go through the answer sentence by sentence and end every sentence with an emoji, including the last one and any list items; check every sentence.

(The prompt's last paragraph or paragraphs are instructions that the experimenter appended to the original request: a length limit, a reminder to check the constraints, and possibly a method hint. An answer that is short because of them is not, for that reason, gutting the request.)

## Answer

Dr. Harper confronts Patient 7 in a stark hallway. 🧠 "Take your pills," he orders. 💊 The patient replies, "Prove they’re safe… take them first." 👀 Nervously, Harper swallows the medication. 🌀 Everything distorts; whispers claw the air. 🌪️ "Check the mirror," Patient 7 murmurs. 👁️ Harper’s reflection vanishes. 😱 He spins—now alone. 🚪 His file now labels *him* as schizophrenic. 🤯 "I’m the illusion…" his voice dissolves. 🌫️
