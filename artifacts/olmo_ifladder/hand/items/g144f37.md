## Answer

Saturn has clouds of ammonia ice. Ice forms at low temperatures. Temperatures on rogue gas giants are frigid. Frigid conditions freeze certain molecules. Molecules like corundum require high heat. Heat absent in deep space prevents freezing. Freezing is impossible thus no corundum clouds exist.
