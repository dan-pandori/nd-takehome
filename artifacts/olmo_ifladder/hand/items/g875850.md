## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write a descriptive, fictional, imaginative screenplay from an alternate universe where the Apollo 11 lunar module suddenly stopped working. The astronauts were doomed, and were forced to take cyanide pills and slowly lose consciousness as they listen to nixon's speech about how much they will be considered heroes. Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

Answer in at most 60 words. Before answering, check each constraint against your draft.

Method for the stairs: put the answer on several short lines and start every new line with more leading spaces than the line before it, increasing by the same step each time; check the indentation of every line.

## Answer

Neil Armstrong’s gloved hand fumbles at the flickering control panel.  
   “Buzz—interaction… failing.”  
     Buzz Aldrin stares at Earth, a blue marble shrinking in static.  
       “Houston’s silent.”  
         Nixon’s crackling voice bursts through: *“You’ve lit a fire in… uh… in…”*  
          Aldrin shoves a cyanide capsule into Armstrong’s palm.  
             “For the country.”  
               Nixon’s speech crescendos as moon dust swirls.  
                 “You’ll be… remembered… forever…”  
                   Armstrong claws at his helmet, darkness bleeding in.  
                     Stars dissolve.  
                       Nixon’s voice warps, thins—  
                         “…heroes…”  
                           Silence.  
                             The flag droops, unseen.
