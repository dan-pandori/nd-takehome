## Answer

Fade In:  

INT. GRAND HALL - DAY  

A CAPTIVATED CROWD WATCHES AS AN ELDERLY MAN STAGGERS TOWARD THE PODIUM. HIS BREATH IS LABORED BUT STEADY. DR. LENA REYES, HOLDING A MICROPHONE, BEAMS. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, WITNESS HISTORY! MR. INT. ETHEL’S BEDROOM - NIGHT  

THE MAN LIES STILL, EYES WIDE OPEN. MOONLIGHT PIERCES THROUGH TARNISHED BLINDS. HIS BREATH SLOW, HE WHISPERS, "QUANTUM IMMORTALITY... THAT THEORY FROM MY YOUTH..."  

FLASHBACK - EXT. UNIVERSITY PARK - DAY (1970S)  

A YOUNG ETHEL SITS ON A BENCH, NOSE BURIED IN A DUSTY BOOK: "QUANTUM IMMORTALITY: THE OBSERVER’S DILEMMA." HE MURMURS, "IF DEATH IS A POSSIBLE BRANCH, WHY AM I HERE? I MUST HAVE CHOSEN LIFE... INFINITE TIMES." HIS LAUGHTER ECHOES, HOLLOW.  

BACK TO PRESENT:  

ETHEL CLUTCHES HIS SHEETS, NAILS DIGGING IN. "ALL THESE YEARS... AND I NEVER DIED BECAUSE I COULDN’T? I’M A CORPSE IN A UNIVERSE THAT REFUSES TO END!" HE SCRIBBLES FRENZIEDLY ON A PAD, INK BLEEDING INTO WORDS. "THE WITNESS COLLAPSED! BUT I PERSIST— A FLAW IN THE COSMOS!"  

HIS VOICE RISES, MANIC. "THIS REALITY IS A LOOP! I’VE LIVED IT EONS— AND I’LL KEEP DYING, ONLY TO REAWAKEN! NO END, NO PEACE!"  

SUDDENLY, THE ROOM SPINS. HIS REFLECTION IN THE MIRROR FADES, REPLACED BY STATIC. A HEART MONITOR BEEPS WILDLY. NURSES BURST IN, BUT ETHEL IS GONE— OR HAS HE ALWAYS BEEN GONE?  

FADE TO BLACK.  

THE END.
