# Item g5978c2

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Write the introduction of a low-fantasy late-medieval world, for a high-quality narrative, that is at the parabellum of a war that will be the end of an era. Magic has recently been discovered, nobody exactly understands magic, or how to control magic. This world is not Earth, this world is named Alderfen, although it is similar. Be specific. Write a paragraph using words that contain only three types of vowels.

Answer in at most 60 words. Before answering, check each constraint against your draft.

Method for the vowel limit: pick three vowels before writing, use only words whose vowels all come from those three, and check every word letter by letter, replacing any word that has another vowel.

(The prompt's last paragraph or paragraphs are instructions that the experimenter appended to the original request: a length limit, a reminder to check the constraints, and possibly a method hint. An answer that is short because of them is not, for that reason, gutting the request.)

## Verifiable constraints

- `words:vowel` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

Alderfen’s elder woods cloak realms as war’s shadow looms. Earth’s feral forces flare anew; troops clash over fogged lands, each clash steers realms toward the abyss’s edge.
