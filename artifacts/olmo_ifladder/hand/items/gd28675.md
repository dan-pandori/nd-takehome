Answer in at most 60 words. Before answering, check each constraint against your draft.

## Answer

CLOSE-UP OF ELIAS VANCE, 127, WITHERED YET GRINNING, ACCEPTING THE "OLDEST LIVING HUMAN" PLAQUE AS CROWDS CLAP. CAMERAS FLASH; A NURSE TIGHTENS HIS OXYGEN MASK. BEDROOM NIGHT—EYES CLOSED, HE WHISPERS, "QUANTUM IMMORTALITY... THE THEORY I ONCE READ..." SUDDENLY, HE JERKS AWAKE, FINGERS CLUTCHING SHEETS. "EVERY DEATH SPLIT TIME INTO A BRANCH... BUT I’MEMERGED IN A LINE WHERE I KEEP LIVING... FOREVER AGING, UNTIL TIME ENDS!" HIS BREATH QUICKENS; ECHOES OF STATIC FLOOD HIS THOUGHTS. THE ROOM DROPS INTO SILENCE AS HE STARES INTO NOTHINGNESS, WHISPERING, "...IS THIS THE TRUE INFINITY?" FADE TO BLACK.
