Each sentence must have a longer sequence of consecutive alliterative words than the previous one. Is it accurate to state that the computations specified by an artificial neural network can involve more than math because activation functions such as relu involve more than just math?

## Answer

neural network computations fundamentally rely on mathematical operations, including activation functions like ReLU, which are defined mathematically. While ReLU introduces non-linearity essential for complex pattern recognition, its implementation—thresholding inputs—is still a mathematical step. Thus, stating computations involve "more than math" is inaccurate; ReLU exemplifies advanced mathematical design, not non-mathematical processes.
