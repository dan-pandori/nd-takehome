## Answer

<i>Kennedy viewed the Space Race as essential for Cold War technological dominance and global ideological influence</i>. He aimed to prove U.S. superiority through bold space missions, surpassing Soviet achievements and securing global trust in democratic ideologies. This drive aimed to not only conquer space but also project U.S. values as the defining standard of 20th-century innovation versus Soviet models globally.
