## Answer

Does the verse ignite the mind?  
Paint vivid pictures bright,  
Original thoughts take flight?  

Rhythm flows, meter true,  
Sense made in lines afresh?  

Heartstrings pluck, emotions told,

Meaning clear, not lost in haze,
