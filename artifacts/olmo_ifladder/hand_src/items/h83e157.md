### Algorithm Design for Generating Unique Magic Abilities in Aethoria  

#### **1. Core Parameters**  
| **Parameter**       | **Description**                                                                 |  
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|  
| **Rank Tiers**      | Common (70%), Uncommon (15%), Rare (8%), Legendary (5%), Mythic (2%)            |  
| **Effect Schools**  | Elemental, Temporal, Soulbinding, Celestial, Arcane, Illusion, Beastmastery     |  
| **Cost Types**      | Mana, Health %, Stamina, Time (cooldown), Permanent Stat Reduction, Soul Fragments, Item Sacrifice |  
| **Consequence Triggers** | Random chance (e.g., 25% for legendary+ abilities)                                   |  

---

#### **2. Algorithm Workflow**  

##### **Step 1: Rank Selection**  
- **Mechanism**: Weighted random selection based on tier probabilities (Common dominates, Mythic is rare).  
- **Output**: Selected rank (e.g., Legendary).  

##### **Step 2: Base Effect Generation**  
- **Process**:  
  - Randomly pick an **effect school** aligned with the rank (e.g., Mythic abilities exclude basic schools like Elemental).  
  - Merge keywords from predefined lists (e.g., *Effect Adjectives* + *Magic Types* + *School Prefix/Suffix*).  
  - Example: *"Celestial"* (school) + *"Orbital"* (adjective) + *"Burst"* → **"Celestial Orbital Burst"**.  

##### **Step 3: Apply Rank-Dependent Modifiers**  
| **Rank**         | **Effect Enhancements**                                                                 | **Baseline Cost** |  
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|  
| **Common**       | Single target, minor duration/AoE.                                                      | 5–15 mana        |  
| **Uncommon**     | Multi-target, moderate duration.                                                        | 15–30 mana       |  
| **Rare**         | Large AoE, lasting effects (e.g., poison), or utility synergy.                         | 30–50 mana       |  
| **Legendary**    | Game-altering effects (e.g., terrain manipulation, targeted vulnerabilities).           | 50–100 mana      |  
| **Mythic**       | Reality-bending, irreversible effects (e.g., time loops, permanent stat boosts/debuffs).| 200+ mana + major permanents |  

##### **Step 4: Cost & Limitations Assignment**  
- **Formula**:  
  - **Base Cost** = Rank² × Tier Multiplier (e.g., *Rank 3*: 3²×5 = 45 base mana).  
  - **Conditionally Apply**:  
    - Health drain: *((Rank - 2)/2)*% HP loss (e.g., Legendary = 1% HP loss per use).  
    - Cooldown = Rank × 5 seconds (e.g., Mythic: 10×5 = 50s cooldown).  
  - **Limitations Table**:  
    - *Common*: 30s cooldown, no resource loss.  
    - *Mythic*: 10% permanent mana reduction, fails if castertarget is immune to magic.  

##### **Step 5: Consequence Generation (for Overpowered Abilities)**  
- **Triggers**: 30% chance for Legendary+, 60% for Mythic.  
- **Mechanism**: Randomly select from a list of severe drawbacks:  
  - *"Age 5 years post-use"*, *"Soul shattered on failure"*, *"Permanent -2 Dexterity"*, *" Attractsplanar rifts for 24h."*  

##### **Step 6: Finalizing Name & Description**  
- **Naming Convention**:  
  **[School Prefix or Adjective] + [Effect Noun] + [Rank-Related Word]** (e.g., *"Abyssal Rift Surge"* for Legendary Earth magic).  
- **Description Template**:  
  *"Knowledge of [Effect Name] permits [primary action]. Costs [costs], limited by [restrictions]. [Optional consequences]."*  
  Example:  
  *"Celestial Paradox Shatter: Shatters spacetime within a 30m radius, dealing 250 damage and banishing foes to a parallel dimension. Requires 150 mana and 12% HP. Casters suffer 3d6 Wisdom damage afterward. (Cooldown: 10m) None may use this twice in one day."*

#### **3. Balance & Uniqueness Checks**  
- **Power-Cost Ratio**:  
  - **Threshold Formula**: Effect power = (Rank ×10) must ≤ (Cost + Consequence Score).  
  - Example: A Legendary 150-damage ability must have costs/consequences summing to ≥150.  
- **Uniqueness Filter**:  
  - Use a hash of parameters (effect school, modifiers, name) to ensure no duplicates.  
- **World Consistency**:  
  - All abilities must align with Aethoria’s lore (e.g., mana sourced from astral planes → "costs cannot exceed max astral intake").
