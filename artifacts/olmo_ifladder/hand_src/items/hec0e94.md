## Answer

- Quantum entanglement is often fictionalized as a means to access or communicate across parallel universes despite lacking empirical basis  
- The manyworlds interpretation involves branching realities but attributes it to quantum superposition not entanglement itself  
- Nonlocal particle correlations in entanglement are metaphorically leveraged in stories to facilitate multiverse travel  
- Fictional narratives may use entangled particles as keys or anchors to sync entrance between dimensions  
- Scientists emphasize no direct link between quantum entanglement and the existence or accessibility of parallel worlds  
- Creative storytelling employs quantum terms like entanglement to add plausibility to multiverse exploration plots  
- Such fictional uses prioritize narrative convenience over adherence to established quantum mechanics principles
