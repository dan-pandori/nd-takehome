## Prompt (as the model received it)

Can you do a complex gloss of the following text using the Leipzig glossing rules?  "Those who end up here have their souls stripped from them, leaving them as a conscious shell. They are left to wander an endless desert under a dying purple sun hanging over the horizon in perpetuality." Every quoted phrase must be followed by an unquoted explanation.

## Answer

"Those who end up here" refers to individuals who involuntarily arrive or find themselves in a specific and undesirable location.  

"have their souls stripped from them" describes the process by which their spiritual essence or life force is forcibly removed through some unspecified means.  

"leaving them as a conscious shell" indicates that, despite the loss of their soul, they retain awareness, existing as an empty, sentient body devoid of vitality or purpose.  

"They are left to wander" signifies the perpetual, aimless movement imposed upon them, suggesting a state of eternal restlessness and torment.  

"an endless desert" establishes the barren and desolate environment in which they are condemned to roam, emphasizing the absence of life or hope.  

"under a dying purple sun" creates a haunting atmospheric condition, with a sun that is fading or expiring, casting a surreal and oppressive light over the landscape.  

"hanging over the horizon" reinforces the sun's fixed position, never setting, which symbolizes the unending nature of their punishment and the denial of change or relief.  

"in perpetuality" concludes the description by stressing the eternal duration of their existence, with no possibility of escape, release, or conclusion.
