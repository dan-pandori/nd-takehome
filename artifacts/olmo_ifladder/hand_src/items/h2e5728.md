For generative neural network AIs, what is memorization, and why should one care about whether memorization occurs? Your response must include bullet points denoted by * and at least one sub-bullet point denoted by - for each bullet point.

## Verifiable constraints

## Answer

* Memorization in generative neural networks occurs when the model reproduces exact or near-exact copies of training data instead of generating novel outputs  
  - This includes regurgitating specific images, text, or patterns from the dataset without abstraction  

* It is important to address memorization because:  
  - It compromises privacy by allowing extraction of sensitive training data through generated outputs  
  - It indicates overfitting leading to poor performance on unseen data and reduced generalization  
  - It raises ethical and legal issues such as copyright infringement for replicated works  
  - It inefficiently uses model capacity favoring memorization over learning generalizable features
