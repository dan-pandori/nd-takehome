What parameters do the viscous friction force depend on, and in which case should it be taken into account for the physical pendulum model? In your response the last word of each sentence must become the first word of the next sentence.

## Answer

Viscous friction depends on several factors. Factors include velocity and fluid viscosity. Viscosity determines the damping coefficient. Coefficient must be considered in physical pendulum models. Models must include it when damping occurs. Occurs when energy loss is observable.
