## Answer

1. The Covenant Of The Steel Plow This Faction Originates From Traditional Amish And Mennonite Communities In Rural Ohio They Reject Modern Technology Relying Instead On Horse Drawn Plows And Manual Labor Their Bases Are Fortified Rural Communes Surrounded By Fertile Farmland In Holmes County And Nearby Areas They Maintain Strict Hierarchies With Elders Leading Communities And Prioritize Self Sufficiency Over Expansion Conflicts Arise With Technology Dependent Groups Seeking To Exploit Agricultural Resources Or Convert Them To Industrial Lifestyles Their Culture Emphasizes Simplicity Mutual Aid And A Deep Connection To The Land  

2. Zion's Holdfast This Fundamentalist Christian Group Emerged From Mega Churches And Right Wing Compounds Across The State They Believe The Apocalypse Was A Divine Judgment And Strive To Create A Theocratic Society Governed By Strict Biblical Law Their Bases Include Repurposed Church Complexes And Protected Farmland In Urban Fringe Areas Like Toledo And Akron They Enforce Harsh Punishments For Sins Such As Questioning Doctrine Or Practicing Nonconformist Beliefs Tensions Escalate With Secular Factions Over Recruitment And Religious Influence Their Culture Blends 21St Century Extremism With Medieval Christian Iconography And Ritual  

3. The Iron Dominion A Trade Empire Controlling Ohio's Rail Network And Key Industrial Hubs Like Cincinnati Formed By Former Engineers Merchants And Survivalists They Maintain Armored Trains To Transport Goods And Enforce Their Authority Across The Region Their Society Values Pragmatism And Hierarchy With A Council Of Technocrats Making Decisions They Often Clash With The Ashen Legion Over Rail Routes And With The Arcanist Collective Over Resource Allocation Their Culture Celebrates Industrial Might With Symbols Like Gears Steam Locomotives And Labor Conventions  

4. The Arcanist Collective A Group Of Scientists And Scholars From University Towns Like Columbus And Oxford They Seek To Rebuild Technology And Preserve Human Knowledge By Establishing Hidden Libraries And Laboratories They Avoid Open Conflict But Will Defend Their Sanctuaries With Advanced But Outdated Weapons And Gadgets Their Relationship With The Iron Dominion Is Tense Due To Disputes Over Access To Technology Internally They Debate The Ethics Of Sharing Knowledge Versus Securing Their Survival Their Culture Blends Academic Formality With A Reverence For Lost Technologies And Speculative Futures  

5. The Ashen Legion A Ruthless Militia Controlling The Ruined City Of Cleveland And Surrounding Territories Formed By Former Gang Members Soldiers And Scavengers They Rule Through Brute Force And Control Of Scarce Resources Like Water And Ammunition Their Leader Known As The Scourge Demands Absolute Loyalty And Frequently Launches Raids On Weaker Communities They Are In Direct Competition With The Iron Dominion For Control Of Critical Infrastructure Their Culture Glorifies Strength Violence And The Mythology Of Postapocalyptic Survival Heroes  

6. The Wandering Hearth A Nomadic Society Of Former Farmers And Ranchers Who Traverse Ohio's Countryside In Armored Caravans And Horse Drawn Wagons They Prioritize Sustainable Agriculture Harvesting Crops And Livestock Without Depleting Land Their Council Of Elders Makes Decisions Based On Consensus And They Rarely Engage In Warfare Preferring To Negotiate Or Flee Conflicts Occur When Settled Groups Attempt To Claim Their Traditional Migration Routes Their Culture Celebrates Seasonal Festivals And Preserves Folklore From Before The War
