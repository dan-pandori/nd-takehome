# Item h5d1033

## Prompt (as the model received it)

If I witness a child being born 5 light years away and then travel to him at the speed of light, how old would the child be from his perspective when I arrive? Create stairs by incrementally indenting each new line.

## Verifiable constraints

- `format:line_indent` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

If I witness a child being born 5 light years away and then travel to him at the speed of light,  
    the light from the birth took 5 years to reach me,  
        meaning the child is already 5 years old when I observe the event,  
            but traveling at the speed of light would cover the distance instantly,  
                resulting in me arriving when the child is still 5 years old,  
                    from their own perspective.
