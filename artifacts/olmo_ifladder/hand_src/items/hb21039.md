# Item hb21039

## Prompt (as the model received it)

Can you do a complex gloss of the following text using the Leipzig glossing rules?  "Those who end up here have their souls stripped from them, leaving them as a conscious shell. They are left to wander an endless desert under a dying purple sun hanging over the horizon in perpetuality." Every quoted phrase must be followed by an unquoted explanation.

## Verifiable constraints

- `format:quote_unquote` {}: followed (official strict checker)

## Answer

"Those who end up here" are a relative clause introducing the subject as individuals arriving at this location.  
"have their souls stripped from them" is a passive construction indicating the removal of their souls through the action of being stripped.  
"leaving them as a conscious shell" functions as a participial phrase illustrating the consequence of the soul extraction resulting in a soulless yet aware entity.  
"They are left to wander" employs passive voice with an infinitive to convey the imposed action of eternal wandering.  
"an endless desert" is a noun phrase specifying the environment in which the wandering takes place characterized by infinite expanse.  
"under a dying purple sun" is a prepositional phrase describing the desert's atmosphere and visual elements emphasizing a decaying and unnatural celestial body.  
"hanging over the horizon" is a present participle phrase modifying sun to denote its suspended position relative to the landscape suggesting perpetual inactivity.  
"in perpetuality" is an adverbial prepositional phrase indicating the scenario's timeless and unending nature reinforcing the eternal quality of their existence.
