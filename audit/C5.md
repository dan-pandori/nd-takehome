## Re-derivation (groups from x0 reads via `n_ok>0`; w1 = worst-step log p at T 1.0)

Every stated number reproduces exactly:
- Groups: A/B/C = 232/54/36, 236/51/35, 234/60/28 at cap 12; 169/101/52, 138/132/52, 140/122/60 at cap 6.
- B eventual w1 at r0 / r8 reproduces.
- Paired-median Δ_RL / Δ_PT per seed: cap 12 is 5.09/3.42, 4.61/4.06, 4.07/4.79. Cap 6 is 9.32/3.59, 8.58/1.11, 8.51/1.84.

**The "1-in-400" figure.** It is exp(−median of B's eventual worst step at r0), at cap 12:

| | s0 | s1 | s2 | pooled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B eventual, median w1 at r0 | −6.58 (1/724) | −6.53 (1/685) | −5.50 (1/245) | −6.33 (1/564); mean of seeds −6.21 (1/495) |
| B reference (selection-free target) | −6.21 (1/499) | −5.76 (1/316) | −5.50 (1/245) | −5.76 (1/316) |
| share of B with w1 < ln(1/400) | 0.61 | 0.63 | 0.43 | |
| share of B with a 2nd step < −4 nats | 0.46 | 0.43 | 0.37 | |
| rest of proof (total − w1), median | −9.2 | −7.2 | −8.2 | |

"About 1-in-400" is a fair middle value: it lies between 1/245 and 1/724 across seeds. Within a seed, though, the interquartile range runs from about 1/180 to 1/17,000.

**"Within one improbable step" is misleading.** At T 1.0, the remaining steps together cost more than the worst step does (−7 to −9 nats against −5.5 to −6.6). About 40 % of B also have a second step below −4 nats.

At cap 6 the same quantity is about 1/22,000 (pooled −10.0). The week-in-review sentence describes cap 12 only.

## Attacks

1. **Group selection.** Defining B from the independent x1 draw moves r0's w1 by at most 0.4 nats (−6.46/−6.46/−5.89). However, 4/9/11 of the x0-defined B theorems are solved by r0 in the x1 draw. So B means "r0 rarely solves it", not "r0 cannot".

2. **"RL concentrated on its own proofs" is partly by construction.**
   - The eventual proof is r8's argmax, so it is selected upward at r8.
   - Selection-free test (new here): score the other cap-12 seeds' eventual proofs under this seed (the cap-6 run's `rev12_s*` files).

   | target (median-of-group) | Δ_RL s0/s1/s2 | Δ_PT s0/s1/s2 |
   |---|---|---|
   | own eventual | 5.30 / 5.19 / 4.26 | 5.21 / 4.07 / 4.45 |
   | other seeds' eventual | 3.44 / 1.76 / 2.52 | 3.53 / 3.37 / 4.02 |
   | reference | 0.79 / 0.98 / 2.42 | 3.82 / 3.19 / 3.35 |

   RL-found proofs from other seeds also gain more than the references, by about 1 nat. But roughly 2.4 nats of the own-proof premium cannot be separated from argmax selection.
   - On these selection-free targets, cap 12's Δ_PT is at least Δ_RL in 3 of 3 seeds. That supports the trajectory title.
   - Generic-training control: the pend replay-only control from rl-from-ckpt *lowers* the B references' w1 (−6.21→−8.34, −5.76→−6.90, −5.50→−6.73), while RL raises it. Caveat: log-p scale differs across models.
   - No control trains on arbitrary sampled proofs. The ladder *is* SFT on the model's own Lean-accepted samples, so "RL" here means expert iteration.

3. **"Several hard steps" (C).**
   - A C reference has a 2nd step below −4 in 0.83/0.69/0.86 of cases, against 0.37–0.46 for B eventual. That is a difference of degree.
   - C is defined by failing at r8.
   - 18 C theorem-seed pairs have no reference proof and are excluded.
   - This sentence is post hoc. The pre-registered expectation ("C stays at one bad step") was **not supported**, and the sentence restates that miss.

4. **Cap-6 vs cap-12 contrast (the difference Δ_RL − Δ_PT).**

   | | cap 6 | cap 12 | Welch test |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | own eventual | 6.27 / 7.55 / 6.54 | 0.09 / 1.12 / −0.19 | t = 11.6, df 4 |
   | references | 4.28 / 6.20 / 2.00 | −3.03 / −2.20 / −0.94 | t = 4.6 |
   | cross-seed | 3.22 / 6.06 / 5.39 | −0.09 / −1.61 / −1.50 | t = 6.0 |

   The contrast also holds on matched theorems (B at both caps, 30–37 per seed): cap 6 gives 6.9–10.0 against cap 12's −0.7–1.8.
   - So the contrast far exceeds the seed spread.
   - Confounds: training set, GPU class, ≈ 1.4–1.5× the ladder compute at cap 12, and headroom. Cap 12's B starts at −6, which leaves at most about 5 nats for RL.
   - Within cap 12, the per-seed theorem-bootstrap CIs of the difference all span 0 (s0 [−0.3, 3.8]; s1 [−1.4, 2.1]; s2 [−1.9, 0.9]).

5. **Pre-registration.**
   - The unresolved headline is trajectory's "B's eventual proof becomes likely mainly in RL". Its falsifier fired in 1 of 3 seeds, and the decision rule needs 3 of 3.
   - The value of w1 at r0 (≤ −5) and "eventual − reference Δ_RL ≥ 1" were pre-registered.
   - The "several hard steps" reading and the selection-free comparisons are post hoc.

**Leakage** (own canonicaliser, renaming plus premise order): 1 of 322 evaluation theorems shares a class with K12 (`textbook_3ed45280…`, which is group A in every seed). None share a class with the cap-6 set or the RL targets.

**Lean.**
- 40/40 random accepted proofs pass (r8 and r0 x0 reads of both runs; pool 2,366).
- Negative controls: 120/120 rejected (wrong theorem 40, final `have` dropped 40, goal replaced by `P ∧ ¬P` 40).
- Caveat: the reads store the environment's ND rendering, not raw tokens.

**Cross-run.**
- Cap-12 eventual proofs re-scored in trajectory-cap6 (`rev12`) equal trajectory's values exactly.
- rl-from-ckpt's re-reads of the same Stage-1 checkpoints (md5 `295929e2` for s0 step 5,000) match trajectory's `n_ok` on 322/322 theorems at every start. They are deterministic replays, not independent draws.

## Rating

- **Run-level findings: holds with caveats.** The "mainly in RL" vs "both" contrast between caps is robust to seeds, targets and matched theorems.
- **Week-in-review sentence: weaker than stated.** "1-in-400" is a median over a very wide distribution. "Within one improbable step" ignores that the rest of the proof costs 7–9 nats. "Own proofs" is partly argmax selection.
- **Suggested wording:** "After pretraining, the median RL-only theorem's hardest step had probability about 1/250–1/700 (3 seeds). The rest of the proof together cost about as much again. RL raised proofs that RL found (in any seed) more than the shortest known proofs."
