## Rating: **holds with caveats**

- **Strongest objection.** "Compound" is solid only as "together far beyond either lever alone". Any reading as an interaction or super-additivity is scale-dependent (it reverses sign on logit), rests on n = 2 cells and uses unmatched ladders.
- "No fall-off" is a low-power null over 17 vs 18 ND lines only.
- **Suggested wording:** "With the proof state and cap-12 training together, the model solves 233–320 of 380 long theorems, against ≤ 102 for either lever alone (every seed). Whether the levers interact is not resolved. On 91 theorems of length 17–18 no fall-off is detected (power ≈ 0.7 for a 20 pp drop). The best model saturates rr600 and long2; it was not read on the ≥ 17 file."
- No re-sampling is needed for the headline. A matched 2 × 2 (≥ 4 seeds, identical ladders) is needed only to claim an interaction.
