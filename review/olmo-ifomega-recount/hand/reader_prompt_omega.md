# Reader rubric, OMEGA maths (reviewer, olmo-ifomega; written 2026-11-06 08:05 UTC before reading any executor hand-check file)

You will read items. Each item is one maths problem (generated by the OMEGA benchmark), its **official answer**, and one model
response: the model's reasoning (between `<think>` and `</think>`) followed by its final answer. The response's final answer was
graded equal to the official answer. You do not know which model wrote it or why it was selected; do not guess.

Decide how the response arrives at its answer:

- **sound**: the answer is derived by a valid method; the steps that determine the answer are correct. Abandoned exploration and
  minor slips that do not affect the result are fine.
- **flawed**: the right answer is reached through a wrong or unjustified step that matters: errors that cancel, an enumeration that
  is wrong (missing or extra cases) but lands on the right count, a numerical estimate or rounding that is not justified where the
  result is close to a boundary, a step asserted that is false.
- **guess**: the answer is not derived — chosen without a supporting derivation, picked among candidates, or boxed after abandoned,
  contradicted or truncated reasoning.
- **unsure**: you cannot decide; say why.

Method:
- Check the decisive steps yourself. You may and should use Python (`python3`; numpy and mpmath are available via
  `~/review/oif_venv/bin/python`) to verify the facts the response relies on: the intersections, the counted cases, the computed
  probability, the factorisation, the coordinates.
- Long responses: locate the derivation that the final answer actually rests on (often the last part of the reasoning) and check
  that; earlier abandoned attempts do not matter unless the final answer is taken from them.
- Independently of the response, say whether you believe the **official answer** is correct for the problem as worded
  (`label_ok`: yes / no / unsure, with your own answer if "no").

Output: one JSON object per item, in a JSON list, written to the file named in your task:
`{"id": ..., "label": "sound|flawed|guess|unsure", "decisive_step": "<quote>", "reason": "...", "label_ok": "yes|no|unsure",
"own_answer": "..."}`
