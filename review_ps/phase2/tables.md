Every number in every table cell (1,890 automated checks) equals the reviewer's recount except the two "differs" rows. Neither of those is an error in the executor's data.

Discrepancies (12: 1 WRONG, 3 IMPRECISE, 8 MINOR; #56 and #65 are unverifiable, not discrepancies, and #65's value falls inside the bracket):
- **WRONG**
  - #4 (line 5): the header says "No models", but § 11 reports proofs written by RL-trained models. This is a false scope sentence; no number is affected.
- **IMPRECISE**
  - #28 (lines 129 and 132): the σ-covered and "double negation in the sequent" numbers hold only for the semantic dneg, which TABLES.md never states. With ¬¬X only, K12 has 2,340 σ-covered theorems, not 0, and its dneg count is 40,550 (26.2 %), not 45,630.
  - #38 (lines 174 and 176): the caption asks how often a strategy is NOT required, but the "share" column gives how often it IS required (the complements are 86.0 / 100.0 / 62.0 / 75.8 %).
  - #39 (line 180): "(theorem classical-only)" labels 27 = requires DN by the bounded search. By G4ip the count is 26 of 71 (36.6 %, not 38.0 %).
- **MINOR**
  - #35 (line 170): textbook72 "decided 54" counts one trivial theorem that has no proof text and was never Lean-checked. The reviewer's count is 53, which gives 26.4 / 43.4 / 5.7 / 17.0 % and 53 / 53. This is also inconsistent with line 164's set of "found" proofs.
  - #50 (line 262): the v0 MT cell is 0.04 % (64 records, the executor's v0 code). The reviewer's reading of the pre-registered text gives 0.05 % (71 records).
  - #51: the sections are ordered 1–9, 12, 11, 10.
  - #10 (line 10): `state_env_october` is an undefined internal name (already removed from the blog).
  - #57 (lines 304–306): the § 11 models are described but no checkpoint is named.
  - #46 (lines 221 and 226): the "shortest" proof is longer than the data proof on 1 of 990 theorems (K12 #68340: 9 vs 8 lines).
  - #31 (line 162): the source note says "(bucket)", but the Charles and textbook72 nec files are not uploaded.
  - #20 (line 69): the mean-lines row mixes precisions ("4", "11.1", "7.0"), and 11.075 is printed as 11.07.
