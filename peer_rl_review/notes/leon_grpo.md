## 5. What worked / what didn't (Leon's own numbers)

**Worked.**
- **Stop instead of redraws** for GRPO + verifier. Redraws collapsed: 13–20 4.4–14.9% [VS:L14, L21]. The stop arm
  trains cleanly [Q:L242–246], but its 3-seed gain over plain GRPO on ynez is not resolved: +0.9 / +2.5 [V:L710].
- **KL anchor 0 in L3.**
  - Dose response at seed 0: 0 > 0.02 > 0.1 [Q:L106–107].
  - Confirmed at 3 seeds on the generated sets: +1.2 to +1.9 pass@1 and +2.4 to +3.4 pass@5 [R:L44–46].
  - Plain reads only; level or negative in guided reads (B2).
- **Net effect of the whole GRPO tuning programme on the long generated sets.**
  - Plain GRPO was behind EI on 13–20: −3.2 / −7.4 (C6). The recipe is level with EI: +0.6 / −0.1 (B4).
  - **derived** from the 13–20 levels (C3 → A1): plain GRPO 28.3 / 50.5 → stop 29.2 / 53.0 → merged
    30.4 / 55.4 → + KL 0 32.1 / 57.8. EI is 31.5 / 57.9.
- **Advantage on human-written sets.** On textbook72 and Charles149 the recipe beats plain EI at pass@1 (+5.6,
  +8.4; B4). But plain GRPO already had about the same edge (+5.6, +7.0; C6), so that edge is a property of GRPO
  in this setup, not of the tuning.
  - Against EI + verifier it holds only on Charles149, plain: +6.9 [+1.0, +13.2] (B6).
  - In guided reads the Charles149 edge over both EI arms is not resolved (B5, B7).
- **Search-trace Stage 1** (T0S, S1g3) is the biggest effect in the project (E2, E3). It is why the v2 levels are
  far above phase 1.
- Single-seed pilots that looked positive: cooldown, 3-premise rows, refill, unlikeliness, weight averaging,
  +2–4 points on 13–20 pass@1 (items 16–22). The 3-seed merged result vs the stop arm is not resolved (+1.2 / +2.4;
  V:L335). The pilot gains did not add up.

**Didn't work** (hurt or within noise):
- Redraw verifier (item 11).
- Unseen-pair prior: −1.7 / −5.6 (item 20).
- In-context feedback: −6.2 / −10.9 on 13–20 (item 25).
- 64 × 64 groups: −5.5 / −5.4.
- Training T 1.2: −2.2 / −3.0.
- 78 L3 iterations, i.e. 2× compute: −1.5 / −1.5, and 2 think-ban rollbacks.
- KL 0.1: −2.7 / −2.2.
- Clip-high 0.4: −1.7 / −1.2 (item 27).
- Half lr (item 13).
- Read at T 1.0 (item 26).
- Curriculum alone, for GRPO (item 10).
- Phase 1: LCPO, budget forcing; GRPO beyond it78 (item 8).
- Within noise or confounded: `x66`, and `p3` (3 canary rollbacks, lr × 0.125) [Q:L100–108].
- hand_proved_14 is at the floor for every arm (A6).

## 6. Leon's own explanations of why

- **Redraw collapse.** "with redraws an invalid step no longer fails the attempt, so the only pressure against it
  is the 0.1 penalty". Marked "Reading (not tested)" [Q:L215–216].
- **No-think stop arm fails on 3-premise problems.**
  - The pools have 0% 3-premise problems, but 13–20 is 78% 3-premise [Q:L258–260].
  - 43% of those attempts end with `S:PR position` at the third premise line [Q:L257–258].
  - The rewrites fix it [Q:L307–310].
- **Late L3 gives half the groups no gradient.** Iterations 30–39 skip 46–51% of groups, nearly all all-solved
  [G:L147–148].
  - The sampler's optimistic prior makes 81–86% of draws first-time pairs [G:L153–156].
  - Motivates the refill, pass-filter and prior pilots [G:L145–170].
- **KL 0.** "The KL dose response suggests that, in L3, an anchor to the L2 model limits how far the policy moves
  toward the long, 3-premise problems. No run tests this directly" [R:L345–347].
- **GRPO sharpens.** Phase 1: "GRPO raises pass@1 a lot and pass@5 not at all, so it sharpens" [RL:L69–70].
- **Unlikeliness reward motivation.** "GRPO loses more at pass@5" [Q:L190].
- **g64 rationale.** "at a 5% pass rate a group of 16 has a success 56% of the time, 64: 96%". It still hurt
  [Q:L101].
- **EI knobs.** ei_think_v's L3 solved 4,989 of 5,000 targets, "so the data knobs may matter more than
  exploration" [Q:L44–45].
- **Stage-1 mechanism.** Lean rejections of environment-accepted proofs fall from 36.9% to 14.0% (hard12) and from
  55.4% to 17.8% (textbook72): "the model learns proofs that check" [O:L179–181].
- **Thinking.** "The thinking traces raise per-attempt accuracy, but … not by enough to pay for about 3× the
  sampled tokens" [TU:L167–170]. With 3 seeds, high-compute uplift appears in 2 of 3 seeds [TU:L313–315].
- **Human-written vs generated split** of GRPO − EI (v2-start arms, 2026-10-13).
  - Pooled human − generated gap: +7.4 [+1.7, +12.3] pass@1 (p 0.011) [LS:L88; SS:L171].
  - Within length bins it is not resolved; 13–20 gives +9.6 [−0.8, +19.2], p 0.066 [LS:L86].
  - It persists after reweighting on goal or atoms [SS:L173–174].
  - So length alone does not explain it, and no single feature does either. No conclusion is drawn.
- **In-context feedback at init.** "feedback does worse than blind resampling at every budget"; the model "often
  copies a dead line's term into its action" [G:L419–421].
- **Run-to-run noise.** "GPU runs are not bit-reproducible; the rerun noise band is about 1–1.5 points"
  [R:L339].

## 7. Protocol details in full

- **Stage 1 (S1g3).**
  - Traces written by code (`searchtrace.py`), no LLM; K12 proofs at levels 0/64/256, mixed 20/40/40; textbook72
    excluded [R:L56–60].
  - The build command passes only `textbook_train.jsonl` via `--extra-exclude`. Per ST20, the dev part is
    covered by `data.DEFAULT_EXCLUDE` [ST20:L411–418; ST20:L164–165].
  - Training: 24k steps on one RTX 3090, 41–60 min a run [S:L133]. Heldout file:
    `/scratch/cowifornia/nd-rl/bench/dan_ckpts/data/heldout.jsonl` [R:L66].
  - The LLM-written trace arm was dropped (safeguard block) [S:L20].
- **GRPO details** beyond P0 [grpo:L1–40; R:L125–159; G:L19–64]:
  - Curriculum sampling: weight p(1−p) + 0.03, with a Beta(1, 1) posterior [G:L153].
  - Loss: token-level mean, normalised by accepted tokens when the step penalty is on [G:L46–50].
  - Rejected draw: in the stop arm, the draw that ends the attempt gets −0.1 on top of the attempt's advantage
    [R:L17–19].
  - Penalty choice: P = 0.1 because at 0.25 the rejected draws carried 58–70% of the gradient mass [G:L66–77].
- **EI arms** [G:L79–108, L472–481, L521–537]:
  - Dan's ladder (`ei.py`), one k-32 round per stage over 5,000 targets, then one 600-step fine-tune (128 records
    a step; ei.py cosine 3e-4 → 3e-5 with a 50-step warmup).
  - `--select shortest`; at most 4 found proofs per target × `--rl_weight` 4; plus 20,000 retained Stage-1
    records. ei_think_v s0's L3 mix was 98,156 records.
  - Sampling T 0.8 (GRPO trains at 1.0). max_steps 96.
  - EI + verifier = `ei.py --arm logical`, ≤ 10 redraws; fine-tunes on accepted steps only [line:L292; G:L63–64].
  - The two methods are matched on sampled attempts only. **derived:** GRPO takes 39 × 4 = 156 AdamW steps a
    stage (G:L112) vs EI's 600 fine-tune steps a round. Training tokens and GPU time are not matched.
  - EI round time: 7,709 s for ei_think_v s0 on ynez; 11,838 s for the slowest (ei_think_v s2) [G:L543–544].
- **Reads** [line:L300–305; G:L708–723].
  - Command: `python -m code.thinking.eval --ckpt <L3 last ckpt> --sets <set> --out <dir> --k <16|64|64|64|8>
    --budgets 64 --arms <plain|logical> --batch 1024 --seed 0 --temperature 0.8 --max_action 512 --max_steps 96
    --max_rej 10`.
  - hand14 uses `--max_steps 160`. k order: t1320, tb72, charles173, hand14, t2130 (`KS=(16 64 64 64 8)`,
    line:L196).
  - The guided read is the logical arm with ≤ 10 rejected steps per attempt [V:L1229–1231].
- **Pools, ynez rebuild** [Q:L166–169; R:L163–170].
  - L2 mean 11.03; L3 13.22 (serrano 13.28). Ex falso rows 13–14% (vs 10%). Preflight passed. Serrano's pools
    could not be rebuilt exactly.
  - L3_3p: 1,843 rows (serrano's build had 1,796 [Q:L284–286]).
  - Pool and eval disjointness checks: prompt, renaming key, and renaming / curried classes against 11 eval sets
    [G:L94–98; G:L513–517].
- **Two "worlds".** Serrano (pre-crash, 10-10/11; VS) and ynez (rebuilt models and pools; V). They are "never
  pooled" [R:L218–220; Q:L170].
- **Hardware and compute.**
  - ynez: 4 × RTX 3090, shared; 2–3 workers [Q:L153]. serrano: "5 × RTX 3090, as on ynez" [Q:L66].
  - ynez job list: "About 84 GPU-h" [Q:L175]. Serrano v2 wave plan: "About 78 GPU-hours" [Q:L203].
- **Rollbacks in the recipe's L3** [R:L331–337]:
  - s0 once (canary at it29, restored to it25, lr halved);
  - s1 twice (think ban at it35 and it38, both restored to it30, so the final weights are it30 + 1 iteration at
    lr × 0.25);
  - s2 none.
  - All three L2 runs had 0 rollbacks.
