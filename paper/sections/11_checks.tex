The pre-registered expectation that doubled caps would cut the cut-off share at least five-fold failed where cut-offs
were large. Most cut attempts are non-terminating, and they stay cut at twice the budget. The counts barely move
so far, so the cut-offs have not hidden successes that matter for any verdict.

guided evaluation proves 58 and 42 on seeds 0 and 1 (seed 2 pending), again with the same families.
